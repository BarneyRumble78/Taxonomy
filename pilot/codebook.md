# Codebook for the placement pilot

Place each claim (one sentence) in four parts.
1. OWNER: the pyramid whose warrant would establish the claim (two-letter code).
2. CELL: the Face O cell at A-level in that pyramid, e.g. PH.O.A3.
3. WARRANT: the warrant type the claim owes (W1–W13).
4. METHOD: the shared method role that would produce it (M1–M7).

Ownership rule: theorem → MA; law of nature → PH; composition and reaction → CH; living function → BI; disorder of humans → MD; Earth state, place, landform → EA; artefact meeting a specification → EN; computation → CS; mind, behaviour, groups → MS; allocation under scarcity → EC; firm practice, trade, tax computation → BU; contest between opposed wills, choice of game → ST; authority and power → PO; legal validity and duty → LA; concept analysis and normative argument → PL; dated human past → HI; language structure and use → LN; works of art and their interpretation → AR; religious claims → RE; learning under an educational arrangement → ED; designed arrangement for human use → DE; production of organisms and food → AG; human pressure and environmental impact → EV; the record and its retrieval → IS; Indigenous knowledge system as a system → IK.
Border rules: incidence is EC, the duty is LA, the computation is BU. Theorem MA, allocation EC, choice of game ST. Delete the arrangement: if the learning claim survives it is MS, if not ED.

Warrants: W1 Proof; W2 Computation with error bound; W3 Controlled intervention; W4 Measurement with uncertainty; W5 Causal identification from observational data; W6 Comparative inference; W7 Source criticism of a dated record; W8 Interpretation traced to a record; W9 Argument; W10 Source plus facts proved before a forum; W11 Verification against a specification; W12 Stipulation or convention; W13 Tradition-internal authority.
Methods: M1 Represent; M2 Derive; M3 Observe; M4 Intervene; M5 Compute; M6 Compare and reconstruct; M7 Verify.
