# Part 7 — Māori-led review pack (prepared, not yet convened)

**What this is.** This is the material a Māori-led review needs: what is being asked, of whom, and on what terms. The review itself has not happened. It cannot be run by the author or by an AI. The placements below stay provisional until it is done, and the ledger marks the IK review gate FAIL until then.

## 1. Purpose
Ask Māori scholars and holders of the relevant mātauranga whether these placements are right, wrong, or should not be made at all. Reviewers may:
- move a claim
- refuse a split, for example by rejecting the one-sentence-one-home rule for mātauranga
- withhold a claim from open classification altogether

Withholding is a valid outcome. Some kōrero is tapu.

## 2. Who should lead it (to be decided by Māori, not by the author)
The author should approach, through proper channels, bodies with standing in this area. Candidates are a Māori research unit at a New Zealand university, Te Mana Raraunga (Māori data sovereignty), and the relevant iwi or hapū rūnanga for any local claim. The author takes no part in choosing reviewers beyond making the invitation.
- **Panel:** at least three reviewers, Māori-majority and Māori-chaired. Add an iwi or hapū representative for any local claim, such as marae kawa or place knowledge.
- **Payment:** reviewers are paid. Koha and fees are budgeted before invitation. Knowledge shared in review stays under Māori control, under the CARE principles and Te Mana Raraunga, and is not published without consent.
- **Aboriginal, Torres Strait Islander and Pacific claims:** these follow the same rule, led by those peoples, in separate panels.

## 4. How the review runs
1. **Invitation and agreement on terms.** Payment, data control and the right to withhold are agreed in writing first.
2. **Material.** The panel gets this pack, the IK pyramid, the ANZSRC 45 note, and the relevant cells of other pyramids.
3. **Hui.** The panel meets, in person or online, at a pace it sets.
4. **Recorded outcomes.** Each placement is recorded as accepted, moved (to where), refused, or withheld. Reasons are recorded only if the panel wishes.
5. **Disagreement.** Where reviewers or iwi disagree, both positions are recorded and the claim stays CONTESTED. The author does not resolve it.
6. **Return.** The revised placements go back to the panel for confirmation before release.

## 5. What happens after
- Accepted placements lose their "provisional" mark.
- Moved placements are applied, and the registry is updated.
- Withheld claims are removed, leaving only a note that a claim was withheld.
- If the panel rejects IK as a pyramid, the file reverts to the ANZSRC 45 scope note, and that decision is recorded in the ledger.
