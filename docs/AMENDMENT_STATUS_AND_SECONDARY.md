# Amendment proposal: Status, and a secondary owner

This page proposes a change. It does not make one. The standard, the engine and the IDs stay as they are until the owner accepts this text.

## What R1 says now

R1 gives every sentence one home. The home is the field whose warrant establishes the claim. Other fields cite that home. They do not own the sentence.

## Status

Add a status on a placement. The four values are current, contested, retracted and superseded.

Status is not a fifth face and not a warrant. It does not move the home. A retracted or superseded sentence keeps its ID. R12 still forbids reuse. The row points at the successor ID when there is one.

Contested records that two homes were argued. It does not create a second owner.

## Secondary owner

MSC practice gives a work one primary code and may add secondary codes. Copy that pattern.

The primary owner remains the field whose warrant establishes the claim. That is still the home under R1.

A secondary owner is a citation. It is written as a uses edge. It does not establish the claim and it is not a second home.

## What this amends

This amends R1. R1 today forbids a second home. The proposal lets a sentence keep one primary home and carry a recorded secondary field, plus a status.

Do not apply it in this pass. Do not add a Status face to the pyramids, the registry, the engine or the API until the owner accepts the amendment.
