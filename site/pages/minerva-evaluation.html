Evaluation

We built a frozen test set of 203 claims and scored our own early classifier against it. The honest baseline: 62.1% on owner field and 37.9% on warrant. That's a starting point, not a finish line. We publish it because a company that asks you not to overstate claims shouldn't overstate its own.
