Meet the founder.

I'm Chris Townsend, founder and CEO of Taxonomy and an AI architect based in New Zealand.

I started Taxonomy because I kept running into the same problem. Smart systems and smart people state things more firmly than the evidence lets them. So I set out to build tools that keep track of the difference.

If that problem matters in your organisation, I can help in four ways:

  • Speaking. Keynotes and workshops on AI, evidence and knowing what you know. Speaking →
  • Writing. Articles, essays and commissioned pieces. Writing →
  • Video. Explainers and talks. Video →
  • Advisory. Hands-on help with AI architecture and evidence-aware systems. Advisory →
