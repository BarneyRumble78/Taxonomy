Meet the founder.

I'm Chris Townsend, founder and CEO of Taxonomy and an AI architect based in New Zealand.

I started Taxonomy because I kept running into the same problem. Smart systems and smart people state things more firmly than the evidence lets them. So I set out to build tools that keep track of the difference.
