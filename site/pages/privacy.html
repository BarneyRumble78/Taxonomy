Privacy

This notice explains what personal information taxonomy.co.nz collects, why, and your rights under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Who we are. Taxonomy is a small business run by Chris Townsend, based in Whanganui, New Zealand. Chris is responsible for privacy matters. Contact: chris@taxonomy.co.nz.

What this website collects

  • No accounts, no tracking cookies, no analytics. The site does not set cookies or run analytics or advertising scripts.
  • The contact and register forms do not send anything yet. What you type stays in your browser and is not delivered to us or stored. We will update this notice before either form starts sending.
  • The classifier tool runs in your browser. Statements you type into the tools on the Minerva pages are processed on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Email. If you email chris@taxonomy.co.nz, the message is forwarded by Cloudflare to a Gmail account (Google). We keep your name, address and message to reply to you and handle your enquiry.
  • The Taxonomy API. If you send text to the Taxonomy API, it is processed to return a result and is not saved by our code. The API does not currently send your text to an AI model; if that changes, we will say so here first.
  • Hosting. The site and API run on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and protect against abuse, under its own privacy policy.

How we use and share it

We use information only to answer you and to run and secure the site. We do not sell it or share it for marketing. Our service providers (Cloudflare and Google) may store data outside New Zealand; we use them because they offer protections comparable to the Privacy Act. We may disclose information where the law requires it.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 and its 13 information privacy principles, you can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Email chris@taxonomy.co.nz. We will respond within 20 working days. We keep enquiry emails only as long as needed to deal with them.

If you are not happy with how we handle your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.
