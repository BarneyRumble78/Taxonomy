# EV — Environmental science

New field, added at the owner's direction. Standard v2 cited, not copied. Why it is here: ANZSRC 41 is not the same as Earth science. A process claim (EA) and a harm claim were forced into one cell.

## 1. SCQA
**Situation.** Environmental science studies coupled human and natural systems: what people do to the environment, what state results, what harm follows, and what is done about it.
**Complication.** EA owns how the Earth works and BI owns how ecosystems work. Nothing owned "this pressure causes this harm to this system", which is the core environmental claim.
**Question.** By what dimension can each coupled claim be placed once, with the process science left to EA, BI and CH?
**Answer.** By the link in the causal chain the claim concerns: pressure, state, impact, response or limit. This is an adaptation of the EEA's DPSIR framework, with drivers folded into pressures and limits added.

## 2. Governing thought
Environmental science is exhausted by four faces of one pyramid: objects, methods, warrants and bridges.
Its objects divide into five links of a coupled system: pressures, states, impacts, responses, and limits.

## 3. Key line
Dimension: **the link in the human–environment causal chain.** Order: causal.
1. Pressures
2. States
3. Impacts
4. Responses
5. Limits

## 4. Face O — Objects

### EV.O.A1 Pressures
**SCQA.** Human activity loads the environment. What load, from what activity? Key line: emissions, land and sea use change, extraction, introduced species.
- EV.O.A1.1 Emissions — greenhouse gases, pollutants. Inventories follow IPCC guidelines (W12 for method).
- EV.O.A1.2 Land and sea use change — clearance, drainage, urbanisation.
- EV.O.A1.3 Extraction — water, fish, minerals.
- EV.O.A1.4 Introduced species — pests and invasives (e.g. possums and mustelids in NZ).

### EV.O.A2 States
**SCQA.** Pressures change the state of air, water, soil and biota. What is the measured state? Key line: air, freshwater, marine, soil, biodiversity.
- EV.O.A2.1 Air quality — the measurement uses CH and EA.
- EV.O.A2.2 Freshwater quality — nitrate, E. coli, sediment.
- EV.O.A2.3 Marine environment — acidification, warming.
- EV.O.A2.4 Soil state — contamination, erosion.
- EV.O.A2.5 Biodiversity status — threat classifications (IUCN Red List criteria, W12).

### EV.O.A3 Impacts
**SCQA.** A changed state harms ecosystems and people. What harm, attributed how? Key line: ecosystem harm, human health impact, attribution, ecosystem services.
- EV.O.A3.1 Ecosystem harm — loss of function and species.
- EV.O.A3.2 Human health impact — the exposure–response is MD.
- EV.O.A3.3 Attribution — the share of an observed change caused by a stated pressure (e.g. event attribution in climate science). The physical model is EA.
- EV.O.A3.4 Ecosystem services — benefits people receive; valuation is EC.

### EV.O.A4 Responses
**SCQA.** Societies respond. Which responses change pressures or states? Key line: restoration, conservation, regulation, monitoring of outcomes.
- EV.O.A4.1 Restoration — measured recovery after intervention.
- EV.O.A4.2 Conservation — protected areas, predator control (Predator Free 2050 is a stated goal, UNVERIFIED name and date).
- EV.O.A4.3 Regulation and instruments — the policy is PO, the price instrument EC, the duty LA. EV owns the measured environmental effect.
- EV.O.A4.4 Kaitiakitanga in practice — guardianship as a response; its tradition-internal meaning is IK.

### EV.O.A5 Limits
**SCQA.** Some states should not be crossed. Where are the limits? Key line: thresholds and tipping points, standards, carrying capacity and boundaries.
- EV.O.A5.1 Thresholds and tipping points — a regime shift occurs past a critical value, assuming bistable dynamics (theory MA.O.A4).
- EV.O.A5.2 Standards and bottom lines — legal or agreed limits (W12; force LA).
- EV.O.A5.3 Carrying capacity and planetary boundaries — the planetary boundaries framework (Rockström et al., 2009) is a proposed set, CONTESTED in its numbers.

## 8. Assignment rules
1. Place by the link in the chain.
2. The process (fluid, chemical, ecological) is EA, CH or BI. The coupled human-caused claim is EV.
3. The policy is PO; the price is EC; the duty is LA.
4. A human health exposure–response is MD.
5. A farming practice is AG; its environmental effect is EV.
