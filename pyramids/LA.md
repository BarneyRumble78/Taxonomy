# LA — Law

## 1. SCQA
**Situation.** Law states what is valid, owed and permitted within a legal system, and how a forum decides it.
**Complication.** v1's governing thought listed four relations ending "order a remedy", while its key line listed five ending "constitute the forum". This breaks R7. v1 also had no first descent.
**Question.** By what dimension can every legal claim be placed once, with all members stated identically?
**Answer.** By the legal relation the claim states. There are five relations: what counts as law, what people owe each other, what follows a wrong, what public power may do, and how a forum decides.

## 2. Governing thought
Law is exhausted by four faces of one pyramid: objects, methods, warrants and bridges.
Its objects divide into five legal relations: sources, obligations, wrongs and remedies, public power, and forums and proof.

## 4. Face O — Objects

### LA.O.A1 Sources
**SCQA.** Before any duty, a legal claim needs a valid source. Systems differ in what they count as law. What is law in this system? Key line: legislation, case law, custom and tikanga, international law.
- LA.O.A1.1 Legislation — statutes and delegated legislation, valid when made by the procedure the system recognises.
- LA.O.A1.2 Case law — precedent binds lower courts in common-law systems (stare decisis); its weight in civil-law systems is lower.
- LA.O.A1.3 Custom and tikanga — in New Zealand, the Supreme Court in Ellis v R (2022) held that tikanga Māori forms part of the common law, as a source to be applied with care (UNVERIFIED wording; Māori-led review required). The tikanga itself, as tradition-internal law, is owned by IK.
- LA.O.A1.4 International law — treaties and custom (ICJ Statute art. 38). Domestic effect depends on the system.
- LA.O.A1.5 Validity — what makes a rule law in a system (for example Hart's rule of recognition). The concept analysis is PL; the claim that a rule is valid here is LA.

### LA.O.A2 Obligations
**SCQA.** Valid law creates duties and rights between persons. What is owed, and by whom? Key line: contract, property, trusts, family and succession.
- LA.O.A2.1 Contract — formation needs offer, acceptance, consideration (in common law) and intention to create legal relations.
- LA.O.A2.2 Property — title, possession, land registration (Torrens in NZ and Australia). Title to goods sold passes under the sale contract and the governing law, not under Incoterms.
- LA.O.A2.3 Trusts and equity.
- LA.O.A2.4 Family and succession.
- LA.O.A2.5 Tax duty — the liability to pay, set by statute. Incidence is EC and computation is BU.

### LA.O.A3 Wrongs and remedies
**SCQA.** When duties are broken, law states the wrong and what follows. Key line: torts, crimes, remedies.
- LA.O.A3.1 Torts — negligence requires a duty of care, breach, causation and damage. New Zealand's accident compensation scheme (ACC) bars most personal-injury suits.
- LA.O.A3.2 Crimes — liability needs the prohibited act and the fault element, unless the offence is strict.
- LA.O.A3.3 Remedies — damages, injunctions, specific performance, sentences.

### LA.O.A4 Public power
**SCQA.** Law also binds the state. What may public power do? Key line: constitutional law, administrative law, human rights, Te Tiriti o Waitangi.
- LA.O.A4.1 Constitutional law — New Zealand has no single entrenched constitution; it relies on statutes such as the Constitution Act 1986, conventions and Te Tiriti.
- LA.O.A4.2 Administrative law — judicial review for illegality, irrationality and procedural impropriety.
- LA.O.A4.3 Human rights — NZ Bill of Rights Act 1990: rights may be subject to reasonable limits demonstrably justified in a free and democratic society (s 5).
- LA.O.A4.4 Te Tiriti o Waitangi (1840) — its legal force depends on incorporation in statute. The Waitangi Tribunal (est. 1975) makes recommendations, mostly not binding.

### LA.O.A5 Forums and proof
**SCQA.** A legal claim is settled before a forum, on facts proved to a standard. What forum decides, and how? Key line: jurisdiction and courts, procedure, evidence and standards of proof, dispute resolution.
- LA.O.A5.1 Jurisdiction and courts — which forum may hear a claim. The NZ Supreme Court replaced Privy Council appeals from 2004.
- LA.O.A5.2 Procedure — pleadings, appeal.
- LA.O.A5.3 Evidence and standards of proof — beyond reasonable doubt in criminal cases; balance of probabilities in civil cases.
- LA.O.A5.4 Arbitration and alternative dispute resolution — awards are enforceable under the New York Convention (1958) in contracting states.

## 8. Assignment rules
1. Place by the legal relation, indexed to a system and a date.
2. Incidence is economics; the duty is law.
3. What law is, as a concept, is PL; what is law in this system is LA.
4. A dated event in legal history is HI; the rule then in force is LA.
5. The political cause of a law is PO.
6. Tikanga as tradition is IK; tikanga as recognised by a state court is LA.
7. A proved fact is settled for the forum only (W10 does not travel).
