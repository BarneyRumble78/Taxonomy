## MA.B.C4
- **Applied mathematical modeling divides into three kinds according to the domain of reality that the models describe and act upon.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from familiar macroscopic physical domains, to extreme-scale fundamental and cosmic physics, to applied systems and decision domains._
  - **Classical mechanics and continuum field theories model macroscopic physical bodies, media, and fields.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from discrete particles to deformable solids, fluids, electromagnetic fields, and heat._
    - Mechanics of particles and systems
    - Mechanics of deformable solids
    - Fluid mechanics
    - Optics, electromagnetic theory
    - Classical thermodynamics, heat transfer
  - **Fundamental and cosmic physics theories model matter, spacetime, and the universe at the smallest and largest scales.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from quantum and statistical foundations to gravity, astronomy, and the Earth._
    - Quantum theory
    - Statistical mechanics, structure of matter
    - Relativity and gravitational theory
    - Astronomy and astrophysics
    - Geophysics
  - **Systems and decision sciences apply mathematics to optimization, social and living systems, control, and information.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from optimization and social decisions to biology, control, and information._
    - Operations research, mathematical programming
    - Game theory, economics, finance, and other social and behavioral sciences
    - Biology and other natural sciences
    - Systems theory; control
    - Information and communication theory, circuits

## MA.O.A3
- **Geometry and topology fields are kinds of mathematical study of space, ranked here from elementary figures to global structure.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure of the taxonomy, from classical geometry to algebraic and differential geometry to topology._
  - **Geometry and convex and discrete geometry are kinds of classical and elementary geometric study of figures, convex sets and discrete configurations.**
    _basis: kinds, order: degree. Ranked from the most elementary field to the more specialized one._
    - Geometry
    - Convex and discrete geometry
  - **Algebraic geometry and differential geometry are kinds of geometry that study spaces through algebraic equations or through calculus and smooth structure.**
    _basis: kinds, order: degree. Ranked from the more concrete smooth setting to the more abstract algebraic setting._
    - Differential geometry
    - Algebraic geometry
  - **General topology, algebraic topology, manifolds and global analysis are kinds of topological and analytic study of the shape and global structure of spaces.**
    _basis: kinds, order: degree. Ranked from foundational point-set topology to the more structured and analytic fields._
    - General topology
    - Algebraic topology
    - Manifolds and cell complexes
    - Global analysis, analysis on manifolds

## MA.O.A4
- **Mathematical analysis divides into two kinds of subfield: the core classical areas that study functions, equations, and transforms directly, and the abstract and applied frameworks that generalize those methods to function spaces and operators or apply them to physical, optimization, and computational problems.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure_
  - **Core analysis areas are the classical subfields that study functions, equations, and transforms directly in real, complex, differential, and harmonic settings.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from real foundations to complex, equation-based, and harmonic analysis._
    - **Real-variable foundations study real functions, measure, integration, and the convergence and approximation of sequences and series.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from basic functions to measure to series and approximation._
      - Real functions
      - Measure and integration
      - Sequences, series, summability
      - Approximations and expansions
    - **Complex and potential-theoretic analysis studies holomorphic functions in one or several variables, together with harmonic potentials and special functions.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from one complex variable to potential theory to several variables and special functions._
      - Functions of a complex variable
      - Potential theory
      - Several complex variables and analytic spaces
      - Special functions
    - **Equation-based analysis studies differential, difference, functional, and integral equations and the dynamics of their solutions.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from ordinary and partial differential equations to dynamics, difference equations, and integral equations._
      - Ordinary differential equations
      - Partial differential equations
      - Dynamical systems and ergodic theory
      - Difference and functional equations
      - Integral equations
    - **Harmonic analysis and transform methods decompose functions into frequency components on Euclidean spaces and on abstract groups, and apply integral transforms.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from Euclidean to abstract settings to integral transforms._
      - Harmonic analysis on Euclidean spaces
      - Abstract harmonic analysis
      - Integral transforms, operational calculus
  - **Abstract and applied frameworks are fields that generalize analysis to function spaces and operators and apply its methods to physical, optimization, and computational problems.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from abstract operator frameworks to physical and computational applications._
    - **Abstract and applied frameworks study function spaces and operators, and apply analytic methods to optimization and applied problems.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from abstract spaces to operators to applied optimization._
      - Functional analysis
      - Operator theory
      - Calculus of variations and optimal control; optimization
      - Applied mathematics
    - **Mathematical physics and numerical and computational mathematics are kinds of research fields that apply mathematical methods and theory to describe physical systems and to solve problems through computation.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure: the theory-oriented field of mathematical physics comes first, then the computation-oriented field._
      - Mathematical physics
      - Numerical and computational mathematics
