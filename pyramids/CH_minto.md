# CH — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Chemistry studies substances, their kinds, their architecture, their energetics and their changes.

**Complication.** The source groups mix library headings, classes of substances, phenomena and methods, so there is no single clear view of how the field is divided.

**Question.** How is everything in chemistry divided when seen as questions asked of a substance?

**Answer.** Chemistry's objects divide by the question asked of a substance into its composition and identity, its structure and bonding, its equilibrium and energy, its rates and mechanisms, and its synthesis and analysis.

## Governing thought

Chemistry's objects divide by the question asked of a substance into its composition and identity, its structure and bonding, its equilibrium and energy, its rates and mechanisms, and its synthesis and analysis.

## Key line

Group of questions about a substance. Basis: kinds. Dimension: the question asked of a substance. Order: structure. The questions run from what a substance is, through how it is built and how it settles and changes, to how it is made or found.

1. Composition and identity is the question of what a substance is, covering its general kind, role and chemical or biological class. `CH.K1`
2. Structure and bonding is the question of how a substance is built, how its parts attract one another and what measurable properties follow. `CH.K2`
3. Equilibrium and energy is the question of how a substance settles into states, exchanges matter and energy and moves between phases. `CH.K3`
4. Rates and mechanisms is the question of how fast a substance changes and by what steps its bonds are rearranged. `CH.K4`
5. Synthesis and analysis is the question of how a substance is made, isolated, detected and measured. `CH.K5`
