# PL Minto groups

## PL.O.A1
- **Philosophy questions are classified by what they are about, whether a specific topic, a situating context, or a general residual heading.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from most specific subject matter, to situating context, to general residual headings._
  - **Subject-matter categories define a philosophy question by the specific philosophical topic or branch it investigates, such as metaphysics, theory of knowledge, or children's philosophy.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from core branches to applied and topical subfields._
    - **Metaphysics and speculative philosophy cover questions about the fundamental nature of reality and being.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Listed from the specific metaphysics heading to the broader speculative and miscellaneous headings._
      - Metaphysics
      - Speculative philosophy
      - M&E, Misc
    - **Core theoretical branches of philosophy ask what exists and how we know it.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from metaphysics alone to its pairing with epistemology._
      - Metaphysics
      - Metaphysics and Epistemology
    - **Philosophy for Children topics apply a particular branch of philosophy, such as ethics, aesthetics, or politics, to children's inquiry.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from value-related branches to reasoning and religion._
      - Philosophy for Children: Aesthetics
      - Philosophy for Children: Ethics
      - Philosophy for Children: Political Philosophy
      - Philosophy for Children: Reasoning
      - Philosophy for Children: Religion and Spirituality
    - **Philosophy for Children works address the method, community, and theory of doing philosophy with children and about philosophy itself.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from method and community to metaphilosophy and specific thinkers._
      - Philosophy for Children: Community of Inquiry
      - Philosophy for Children: Educational Theory and Methods
      - Philosophy for Children: Metaphilosophy
      - Philosophy for Children: Specific Philosophers
    - **Topical subfields take up a specific philosophical subject, such as intentional action or religious belief.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure of topic: action first, then religion._
      - Philosophy of Action
      - Philosophy of Religion
  - **Contextual categories define a philosophy question by the historical era, region, cultural tradition, or educational setting in which the thought is situated.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from historical and regional contexts to educational ones._
    - **Historical-period philosophy covers questions about the thinkers and ideas of a particular era of Western thought.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Listed in chronological order of era, from ancient to twentieth century._
      - Ancient Greek and Roman Philosophy
      - Medieval and Renaissance Philosophy
      - 17th/18th Century Philosophy
      - 19th Century Philosophy
      - 20th Century Philosophy
    - **Regional and cultural tradition philosophy covers questions about the philosophical thought of a specific geographic or cultural tradition.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Listed from European traditions to non-Western and comparative traditions._
      - European Philosophy
      - Continental Philosophy
      - Asian Philosophy
      - African/Africana Philosophy
      - Philosophy of specific cultures (incl. comparative philosophy)
    - **Contextual areas are defined by a region or an educational activity rather than by a single philosophical topic.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure of context: region first, then teaching._
      - Philosophy of the Americas
      - Teaching Philosophy, Misc
    - **Introductory and school-based works present philosophy to newcomers and place it in educational settings.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from introductions to schools._
      - Philosophy for Children: Introductions
      - Philosophy in Schools
      - Philosophy, Introductions and Anthologies
      - Philosophical Traditions
  - **General and residual categories are broad or catch-all headings for philosophy material that fits no more specific topic or context.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from broad top-level headings to residual collections._
    - **General philosophy categories are broad top-level headings that cover philosophy as a whole or its Western history.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Listed from the broadest general headings to the history of Western philosophy._
      - Philosophy (General)
      - Philosophy
      - History of Western Philosophy
    - **Residual categories collect philosophy and religious studies questions that fit no more specific heading.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Listed from philosophy-specific leftovers to the broader other-studies leftovers._
      - History of Western Philosophy, Misc
      - Philosophy not elsewhere classified
      - Other philosophy and religious studies
      - Other philosophy and religious studies not elsewhere classified
    - **General and miscellaneous works collect philosophy material that fits no specific topic.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from general works to miscellaneous catch-alls._
      - Philosophy, General Works
      - Philosophy, Misc
      - Philosophy, Miscellaneous
      - Philosophical Traditions, Miscellaneous
      - Philosophy for Children, Misc

## PL.O.A2

  _basis: kinds, order: structure_

    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Both entries name the same knowledge-focused field, listed by source._
    - Epistemology
    - Epistemology
  - **Phenomenology and philosophical psychology are fields of philosophy defined by their focus on the mind, conscious lived experience, and the sources of human action and moral thought.**

  - **Philosophy of mind covers questions about the nature of mind, cognition, conscious experience, mental life and action.**

    - Psychoanalytic philosophy
    - Philosophy of Mind
