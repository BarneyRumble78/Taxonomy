# IK Minto groups

## IK.O.A1
- **Aspects of an Indigenous knowledge system are the distinct areas a claim can be about, spanning understanding of the natural world, technology and design, and the management of knowledge itself.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure_
  - **Natural-world science aspects concern Indigenous understanding of the sky, living things, genes, matter and quantity.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from the heavens to living things to matter and quantity._
    - **Natural-world knowledge fields cover Indigenous understanding of the sky, living things, genes, and physical and chemical phenomena.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the sky, to living things, to genes, to the physical and chemical sciences._
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander astronomy and cosmology
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander biological sciences
      - Te mātai arorangi me te mātai tuarangi o te Māori (Māori astronomy and cosmology)
      - Ngā pūtaiao koiora o te Māori (Māori biological sciences)
      - Te mātai huingaira o te Māori (Māori genomics)
    - **Natural-world science aspects concern the study of the heavens, living things, matter and quantity within Māori and Pacific Peoples knowledge systems.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure_
      - Ngā pūtaiao pāngarau, ōkiko, matū hoki - hāunga te mātai arorangi me te mātai tuarangi o te Māori (Māori mathematical, physical and chemical sciences - excl. astronomy and cosmology)
      - Pacific Peoples astronomy and cosmology
      - Pacific Peoples biological sciences
      - Pacific Peoples genomics
      - Pacific Peoples mathematical, physical and chemical sciences (excl. astronomy and cosmology)
  - **Technology and design aspects concern how Indigenous peoples build structures, compute, and create new ideas and practices.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from engineered structures and computing tools to the creation of new things._
    - **Built technology fields cover how Indigenous peoples design and use engineered structures and computing tools.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from computing technology to engineering, with Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander items before Māori items._
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander computing technology use and design
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander engineering
      - Te whakamahi me te hoahoa o te hangarau rorohiko o te Māori (Māori computing technology use and design)
      - Te mātauranga pūkaha o te Māori (Māori engineering)
    - **Technology and design aspects concern how Pacific Peoples build, compute and create new things using their knowledge.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure_
      - Pacific Peoples computing technology use and design
      - Pacific Peoples engineering
      - Pacific Peoples innovation
    - **Innovation fields cover the creative generation of new ideas and practices within Indigenous knowledge systems.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander innovation is listed before Māori innovation._
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander innovation
      - Te wairua auaha o te Māori (Māori innovation)
  - **Knowledge management and umbrella aspects concern how knowledge and data are held, organised, governed and passed on, together with the top-level and catch-all headings.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from the governance and handling of knowledge to the overarching and residual categories._
    - **Information and knowledge governance fields cover how Indigenous knowledge and data are held, managed, and controlled.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from information systems, to management methods, to data sovereignty._
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander information and knowledge management systems
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander knowledge management methods
      - Ngā pūnaha whakahaere mōhiohio me te mātauranga o te Māori (Māori information and knowledge management systems)
      - Te mana motuhake o ngā raraunga Māori (Māori data sovereignty)
    - **Knowledge management aspects concern how knowledge is organised, held, stored and passed on within Māori and Pacific Peoples knowledge systems.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure_
      - Ngā tikanga whakahaere mōhiotanga Māori (Māori knowledge management methods)
      - Pacific Peoples information and knowledge management systems
      - Pacific Peoples knowledge management methods
    - **Whole Indigenous science traditions are top-level knowledge systems, treated as complete bodies of knowledge rather than as single subject areas.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Top-level fields, each covering an entire Indigenous knowledge system._
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander sciences
      - Ngā pūtaiao Māori (Māori sciences)
    - **Specific subject areas within Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander sciences are subfields that pick out one aspect of that knowledge system, such as genomics, mathematical and physical sciences, or unclassified topics.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Subfields nested within the Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander sciences field._
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander sciences not elsewhere classified
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander mathematical, physical and chemical sciences (excl. astronomy and cosmology)
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander genomics
    - **Residual and umbrella categories cover Māori and Pacific Peoples sciences as a whole or those not placed in a more specific field.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure_
      - Ngā pūtaiao Māori kāore anō kia whakarōpūhia i wāhi kē (Māori sciences not elsewhere classified)
      - Pacific Peoples sciences
      - Pacific Peoples sciences not elsewhere classified

## IK.O.A2
- **The aspect of a knowledge system that a claim addresses is determined by how its categories divide environmental knowledge and by whether it concerns knowing or doing.**
  _basis: parts, order: structure. Parts are arranged from the classification scheme of the knowledge to its orientation toward knowing or doing._
  - **Aspect categories are the specific, general and top-level divisions used to classify environmental knowledges of Indigenous peoples.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from specific domains (land, stewardship, marine) to general and top-level divisions._
    - **Land-based production and earth knowledge covers how Indigenous peoples cultivate, harvest forests, and understand the physical earth.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from cultivated land use to earth science, across Aboriginal, Māori and Pacific fields._
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander agriculture and forestry
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander earth sciences
      - Te ahuwhenua me ngā mahi ngahere o te Māori (Māori agriculture and forestry)
      - Ngā pūtaiao-ā-nuku o te Māori (Māori earth sciences)
      - Pacific Peoples agriculture and forestry
    - **Stewardship knowledge concerns the conservation of the environment and the management of land and water resources.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from conservation to land and water management, Aboriginal then Māori._
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander environmental conservation
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander land and water management
      - Te whāomoomo taiao o te Māori (Māori environmental conservation)
      - Te whakahaere whenua me te wai o te Māori (Māori land and water management)
    - **Marine knowledge concerns the sea environment and the customary harvesting of fisheries.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from fisheries to marine environment science, Aboriginal then Māori._
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander fisheries and customary fisheries
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander marine environment science
      - Te ahumoana me te ahumoana tuku iho o te Māori (Māori fisheries and customary fisheries)
      - Te pūtaiao taiao moana o te Māori (Māori marine environment science)
    - **General and residual knowledge covers environmental knowledges as a whole and items not classified under a more specific aspect.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from general knowledges to not elsewhere classified, Aboriginal then Māori._
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander environmental knowledges
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander environmental knowledges and management not elsewhere classified
      - Ngā mātauranga taiao o te Māori (Māori environmental knowledges)
      - Ngā mātauranga taiao o te Māori kāore anō kia whakarōpūtia i wāhi kē (Māori environmental knowledges not elsewhere classified)
    - **Top-level fields are the broad umbrella divisions of environmental knowledges for each Indigenous peoples' knowledge system.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander, Māori, then Pacific Peoples._
      - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander environmental knowledges and management
      - Ngā mātauranga taiao o te Māori (Māori environmental knowledges)
      - Pacific Peoples environmental knowledges
  - **Orientation fields distinguish whether Pacific Peoples' environmental knowledge is about what they know or about what they actively do.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure: knowledge-focused fields first, then practice-focused fields._
    - **Knowledge-focused fields describe what Pacific Peoples know and understand about the earth, the environment and the sea.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure: general environmental knowledge, then earth and marine domains, then the residual category._
      - Pacific Peoples environmental knowledges
      - Pacific Peoples earth sciences
      - Pacific Peoples marine environment science
      - Pacific Peoples environmental knowledges not elsewhere classified
    - **Practice-focused fields concern how Pacific Peoples actively conserve, harvest and manage environmental resources.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure: conservation, then resource use in fisheries, then land and water management._
      - Pacific Peoples environmental conservation
      - Pacific Peoples fisheries and customary fisheries
      - Pacific Peoples land and water management

## IK.O.A3
- **The aspect of a knowledge system that a claim is about can be health and community, Māori and general social systems, or Pacific governance and scholarship.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure of the knowledge system: health and community, then Māori and general social systems, then Pacific governance and scholarship._
  - **Health and community aspects cover the peoples, cultural context, science and service delivery through which Indigenous and Pacific health and wellbeing are understood and supported.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds are listed by sub-domain, not ranked._
    - **Communities and cultural context aspects concern the peoples who hold the knowledge and the cultural, life-stage, emotional and spiritual dimensions of their wellbeing.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from the peoples themselves to the cultural and wellbeing contexts they live in._
      - **Indigenous peoples and Maori name the communities who hold, carry and are the subject of the knowledge system.**
        _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the general category of Indigenous peoples to the specific example of Maori._
        - Indigenous peoples
        - Maori
      - **Cultural determinants, lifecourse, child, men's, and mothers and babies wellbeing describe the cultural and life-stage context within which health is understood.**
        _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from culture as a foundation to specific life stages and population groups._
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander cultural determinants of health
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander lifecourse
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander child health and wellbeing
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander men’s health and wellbeing
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander mothers and babies health and wellbeing
      - **Fields concerned with wellbeing and health practice, covering social, emotional, cultural, spiritual, youth, family and physical activity aspects.**
        _basis: kinds, order: degree. Ranked from general wellbeing to more specific population groups and activities._
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander social, emotional, cultural and spiritual wellbeing
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander youth and family social and emotional wellbeing
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander sport and physical activity
      - **Wellbeing and psychology fields concern Māori minds, knowledge systems and the emotional, cultural and spiritual dimensions of wellbeing.**
        _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from the discipline of psychology to its knowledge systems and broader wellbeing._
        - Mātauranga hinengaro kaupapa Māori (Māori psychology)
        - Ngā pūnaha mātauranga hinengaro o te Māori (Māori psychology knowledge systems)
        - Te oranga ā-pāpori, ā-hinengaro, ā-ahurea, ā-wairua o te Māori (Māori social, cultural, emotional and spiritual wellbeing)
    - **Health science and population study aspects concern the biomedical, epidemiological and disease-pattern knowledge used to understand Indigenous health.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from general scientific fields to Māori-specific and population-level fields._
      - **Biomedical science, medicine and treatments, epidemiology, diet and nutrition, and psychology are the bodies of health knowledge and scientific inquiry.**
        _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from biomedical foundations to applied and population-level science._
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander biomedical and clinical sciences
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander medicine and treatments
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander epidemiology
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander diet and nutrition
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander psychology
      - **Fields that study Māori health and wellbeing, covering disability, biomedical science and cultural determinants of health.**
        _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from the overarching health field to its specific subfields._
        - Te hauora me te oranga o te Māori (Māori health and wellbeing)
        - Te whaikaha me te Māori (Māori and disability)
        - Ngā pūtaiao koiora-hauora, haumanu hoki o te Māori (Māori biomedical and clinical sciences)
        - Ngā tokoingoa ahurea o te hauora o te Māori (Māori cultural determinants of health)
      - **Population-level health fields study and act on the health of Māori communities through diet, disease patterns, policy, promotion and public health.**
        _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from the study of health patterns to the policies and programmes that act on them._
        - Te horakai me ngā kai o te Māori (Māori diet and nutrition)
        - Te mātai tahumaero o te Māori (Māori epidemiology)
        - Ngā kaupapahere hauora o te Māori (Māori health policy)
        - Te whakatairanga hauora o te Māori (Māori health promotion)
        - Te hauora me te oranga tūmatanui o te Māori (Māori public health and wellbeing)
    - **Practice and system delivery aspects concern the services, professions, policies and conditions through which health knowledge is applied and care is delivered.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from services and workforce to system frameworks and shaping conditions._
      - **Health services, nursing, midwifery and paediatrics, and disability cover the practice, workforce and care delivery through which knowledge is applied.**
        _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from service systems to the professions and care contexts within them._
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander health services
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander nursing
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander midwifery and paediatrics
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander and disability
      - **Overall health and wellbeing, health policy, health promotion, and public health are the system-level frameworks that govern and promote population health.**
        _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the overarching field to policy and population-level action._
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander health and wellbeing
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander health policy
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander health promotion
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander public health and wellbeing
      - **Health-focused fields address the health and wellbeing of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples, including remote contexts, social determinants, and other unclassified topics.**
        _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure: specific health fields first, the not-elsewhere-classified residual last._
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander remote health
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander social determinants of health
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander health and wellbeing not elsewhere classified
      - **Design-focused fields address how Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander knowledge shapes architecture and the practice and management of design.**
        _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure: design practice and management, then architecture._
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander design practice and management
        - Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander architecture
      - **Life-stage and care-role fields concern the health of Māori at particular points in life and the professions that provide that care.**
        _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from the life course overall to specific stages and the professions that serve them._
        - Ngā wāhanga ora o te Māori (Māori life course)
        - Ngā kaiwhakawhānau me te mātai mate tamariki o te Māori (Māori midwifery and paediatrics)
        - Te hauora me te oranga ā-whaea, ā-pēpi o te Māori (Māori mothers and babies health and wellbeing)
        - Te mahi tapuhi o te Māori (Māori nursing)
    - **Pacific health knowledge covers the science, cultural determinants, population groups, professional settings and delivery of care that bear on Pacific peoples' health and wellbeing.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from foundations and determinants to populations, settings and delivery._
      - **Pacific health foundations and science covers the scientific, cultural and population evidence that explains Pacific peoples' health conditions.**
        _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure of evidence, from conditions and biomedical science to determinants, nutrition and epidemiology._
        - Pacific Peoples and disability
        - Pacific Peoples diet and nutrition
        - Pacific Peoples epidemiology
        - Pacific Peoples health and wellbeing
      - **Pacific Peoples health fields are kinds of knowledge distinguished by whether a claim addresses cultural determinants of health or biomedical and clinical science.**
        _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure: cultural determinants first, then biomedical and clinical science._
        - Pacific Peoples cultural determinants of health
        - Pacific Peoples biomedical and clinical science
      - **Pacific Peoples health fields are the professional and service settings, such as nursing, public health and remote care, in which health care and public health for Pacific communities are practised.**
        _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure of the classification codes._
        - Pacific Peoples nursing
        - Pacific Peoples public health and wellbeing
        - Pacific Peoples remote health
        - Pacific Peoples health and wellbeing not elsewhere classified
      - **Pacific health delivery and care covers the policies, promotion and clinical care through which health services reach Pacific peoples across life stages.**
        _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure of delivery, from policy and promotion to life course, treatment and maternal-child care._
        - Pacific Peoples health policy
        - Pacific Peoples health promotion
        - Pacific Peoples life course
        - Pacific Peoples medicine and treatments
        - Pacific Peoples midwifery and paediatrics
