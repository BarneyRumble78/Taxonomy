# MS Minto groups

## MS.O.A1
- **Categories of psychology claims are distinguished by the unit the claim is about: the whole field, the individual mind, or an applied setting.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the whole discipline to the individual mind to applied settings._
  - **Whole-field categories take psychology itself, as a discipline, its boundaries, or leftover topics, as the unit that claims are about.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the discipline as a whole to its edges and remainders._
    - **General psychology covers the discipline as a whole, including its overall scope and its history and systems.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the general field to its historical framing._
      - General Psychology
      - History & Systems
      - Animal Experimental & Comparative Psychology
      - Learning & Motivation
      - Social & Instinctive Behavior
    - **Psychology's boundary topics link it to the humanities, paranormal claims, literature and the arts, and philosophy.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the paranormal boundary topic and the general humanities category to specific fields._
      - Psychic Phenomenon & Paranormal Activities
      - Psychology & the Humanities
      - Literature & Fine Arts
      - Philosophy
    - **Residual categories collect psychology topics that fit no named category, so the unit is whatever is left over.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from the broader residual class to its not-elsewhere-classified leaf nodes._
      - Cognitive and computational psychology not elsewhere classified
      - Other psychology
      - Other psychology not elsewhere classified
    - **Whole-field labels treat psychology itself, as a discipline or phenomenon class, as the unit the claim is about.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from the broadest field heading to the narrower phenomena class._
      - Psychology
      - Psychiatry and Psychology
      - Psychological Phenomena
  - **Mind-centred categories take the individual's own mental processes and inner experience as the unit that claims are about.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from general experimental study to specific processes and inner states._
    - **Human experimental psychology studies the mental and bodily processes of people through controlled research on perception, movement, thought, emotion and awareness.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the general category to input, output, higher mental and conscious processes._
      - Human Experimental Psychology
      - Sensory Perception
      - Motor Processes
      - Cognitive Processes
    - **Consciousness states and motivation and emotion are kinds of internal mental processes that psychology studies in the individual person.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Two parallel kinds of internal mental process; no ranking is implied._
      - Consciousness States
      - Motivation & Emotion
    - **Cognitive topics treat a specific mental process or the intelligent systems built around it as the unit the claim is about.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, starting with the umbrella field and then its component processes._
      - Cognitive Psychology & Intelligent Systems
      - Cognitive and computational psychology
      - Cognition
      - Decision making
      - Learning, motivation and emotion
    - **Further cognitive process categories name memory, language and sensory functions as the unit the claim is about.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from memory and language to sensory processes._
      - Memory and attention
      - Psycholinguistics (incl. speech production and comprehension)
      - Sensory processes, perception and performance
  - **Applied-setting categories take practical contexts and built environments in which psychological knowledge is used as the unit that claims are about.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from general applied practice to the physical environment._
    - **Applied psychology uses psychological knowledge in practical settings such as sport, recreation, engineering and the designed environment.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from sport and leisure applications to engineering applications._
      - Sport Psychology & Leisure
      - Sports & Exercise
      - Recreation & Leisure
      - Engineering & Environmental Psychology
      - Human Factors Engineering
    - **Applied topics treat the physical or built environment and the people's settings in it as the unit the claim is about.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from the smallest designed setting to the widest societal system._
      - Lifespace & Institutional Design
      - Community & Environmental Planning
      - Environmental Issues & Attitudes
      - Transportation

## MS.O.A2
- **The unit a biological claim is about, whether the inner workings of the individual organism or the behaviour that organism produces, separates two kinds of fields.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Fields are sorted by the unit their claims concern: internal organism structure and process versus behaviour and its origins._
  - **Fields whose claims concern the structures, systems, methods or processes inside the individual organism, from whole-discipline headings to specific bodily mechanisms.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from broadest umbrella fields to anatomical parts, functional systems, methods and internal processes._
    - **Broad umbrella fields are top-level disciplinary headings whose claims concern the whole of brain science or biological psychology.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. ordered by structure_
      - Physiological Psychology & Neuroscience
      - Neurosciences
      - Biological psychology
      - Neurosciences not elsewhere classified
    - **Anatomical divisions of the nervous system are claims about a particular part of the nervous system, such as central, peripheral, autonomic or cellular.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. ordered by structure_
      - Autonomic nervous system
      - Cellular nervous system
      - Central nervous system
      - Peripheral nervous system
    - **Functional and clinical systems are claims about a specific function or disorder, such as sensory processing, neurological disease or physiological processes.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. ordered by structure_
      - Sensory systems
      - Neurology and neuromuscular diseases
      - Neuropsychology & Neurology
      - Physiological Processes
    - **Measurement and modelling approaches are claims about the methods used to record, intervene in or simulate neural and bodily activity.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. ordered by structure_
      - Electrophysiology
      - Psychophysiology
      - Psychopharmacology
      - Computational neuroscience (incl. mathematical neuroscience and theoretical neuroscience)
    - **Fields of psychological science whose claims concern processes inside the individual organism, such as how drugs act, how bodily signals relate to mind, and which brain systems underlie social and emotional function.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from drug-body interaction to bodily signals to brain systems._
      - Psychopharmacology
      - Psychophysiology
      - Social and affective neuroscience
  - **Fields whose claims concern behaviour and its origins, linking genes, neural mechanisms, cognition and evolved adaptations to what organisms do.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from individual-level behavioural links to species-level evolutionary origins._
    - **Behaviour-linked subfields are claims about how genes, neural mechanisms and cognition relate to behaviour.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. ordered by structure_
      - Genetics
      - Behavioural genetics
      - Behavioural neuroscience
      - Cognitive neuroscience
    - **Evolutionary psychological studies make claims about the species-level, evolved origins of mind and behaviour, treating adaptations across a population as the unit of analysis.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Single species-level field grouped with the residual category because its claims do not target a specific individual-level system._
      - Evolutionary psychological studies
      - Biological psychology not elsewhere classified

## MS.O.A3
- **Psychology topic categories are sorted by the unit their claims are about: the subject matter studied, the practice applied to people, or the taxonomy heading used to classify the field.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure_
  - **Core subfields and conditions are areas of psychological study whose claims concern methods of measurement, human change and individuality, and the disorders and exposures that affect health.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from methods to development to personality to disorders to exposures._
    - **Psychometrics and methodology covers the tools and procedures used to measure, analyze, and study psychological phenomena.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Broad field first, then its subfields._
      - Psychometrics & Statistics & Methodology
      - Tests & Testing
      - Statistics & Mathematics
      - Research Methods & Experimental Design
      - Health & Mental Health Treatment & Prevention
    - **Developmental psychology covers how people change across the lifespan in thinking, perception, social growth, and aging.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Broad field first, then its subfields._
      - Developmental Psychology
      - Cognitive & Perceptual Development
      - Psychosocial & Personality Development
      - Aging & Older Adult Development
    - **Personality psychology covers the stable traits and processes that characterize individuals and the theories that explain them.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Broad field first, then its subfields._
      - Personality Psychology
      - Personality Traits & Processes
      - Personality Theory
    - **Disorders cover the psychological, behavioral, developmental, communication, and physical conditions that impair functioning or health.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Broad category first, then its specific disorder types._
      - Psychological & Physical Disorders
      - Psychological Disorders
      - Eating Disorders
      - Communication Disorders
      - Physical & Somatic Disorders
    - **Environmental toxins and the behavioral and neurodevelopmental conditions they are studied alongside make up the health-related subject areas that a claim can be about.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Members are listed structurally: the exposure area first, then the two clinical disorder areas; no ranking by degree is implied._
      - Environmental Toxins & Health
      - Behavior Disorders & Antisocial Behavior
      - Neurodevelopmental & Autism Spectrum Disorders
  - **Applied practice and care fields are those whose claims concern how psychology is used on people in treatment methods, care settings, life stages, real-world arenas, and clinical professions.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the intervention to the setting to the people to the arena to the profession._
    - **Clinical interventions in psychology are distinct treatment approaches that differ by method: talk-based therapy, medication-based treatment, and specialized targeted techniques.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure of the classification: general talk-based therapy, then drug-based treatment, then specialized interventions._
      - Psychotherapy & Psychotherapeutic Counseling
      - Clinical Psychopharmacology
      - Specialized Interventions
    - **Care-setting fields make claims about the system or context in which health, mental health, rehabilitation or military care is delivered.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from general health to services to rehabilitation to the military setting._
      - Health Psychology & Medicine
      - Health & Mental Health Services
      - Rehabilitation
      - Military Psychology
    - **Life-stage and learner fields make claims about people defined by age or educational status, such as children, students and older adults.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from childhood to schooling to later life._
      - Child and adolescent development
      - Educational psychology
      - Psychology of ageing
    - **Applied-practice domain fields make claims about behavior within a specific real-world arena such as law, work or sport.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from legal to workplace to athletic arenas and the residual applied category._
      - Forensic psychology
      - Industrial and organisational psychology (incl. human factors)
      - Sport and exercise psychology
      - Applied and developmental psychology not elsewhere classified
      - Applied and developmental psychology
    - **Measurement and clinical-profession fields make claims about how psychological attributes are assessed and analyzed or how clinicians evaluate and treat individuals.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from methodology to testing to clinical specialties._
      - Psychological methodology, design and analysis
      - Testing, assessment and psychometrics
      - Clinical and health psychology
      - Clinical neuropsychology
      - Clinical psychology
  - **Classification-scheme categories are headings from formal research taxonomies whose claims concern a discipline or a top-level domain of behavior.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the ANZSRC discipline classification to the MeSH behavior heading._
    - **Clinical and health psychology fields are ANZSRC research classifications whose claims concern a discipline of psychological practice and study.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. n/a_
      - Counselling psychology
      - Health psychology
      - Clinical and health psychology not elsewhere classified
    - **Behavior and human activity categories are MeSH top-level headings whose claims concern what people do and the mechanisms behind it.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. n/a_
      - Behavior and Behavior Mechanisms
      - Human Activities

## MS.O.A5
- **Social science fields are classified by the unit their claims are about, whether individuals, groups, organizations, populations, or whole societies.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure_
  - **Work and personnel fields take professionals, individual workers and their jobs, or whole organizations as the unit their claims are about.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from individual professionals and workers up to whole organizations._
    - **Health and mental health personnel issues concern the professionals who deliver care, including their education, attitudes, ethical standards, and impairment.**
      _basis: parts, order: structure. From the general category to specific aspects of professional life._
      - Health & Mental Health Personnel Issues
      - Professional Education & Training
      - Professional Personnel Attitudes & Characteristics
      - Professional Ethics & Standards & Liability
      - Professional Impairment
    - **Individual workers and their jobs are the unit when claims address career interests, selection and training, performance evaluation, and job attitudes.**
      _basis: parts, order: structure. Follows the worker from career choice through selection to performance and satisfaction._
      - Occupational Interests & Guidance
      - Personnel Management & Selection & Training
      - Personnel Evaluation & Job Performance
      - Personnel Attitudes & Job Satisfaction
    - **Organizations as whole systems are the unit when claims address the field itself, management, organizational behavior, and working conditions.**
      _basis: parts, order: structure. From the field as a whole to management and workplace conditions._
      - Organizational Psychology & Human Resources
      - Management & Management Training
      - Organizational Behavior
      - Working Conditions & Industrial Safety
      - Social Structure & Organization
  - **Anthropology fields study human societies and cultures, each taking a different unit, such as the individual person or a cultural form, as the subject of its claims.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from the discipline and its subfields to the individual-person and cultural-unit fields._
    - **Anthropology and its subfields study human societies and cultures, with each subfield taking a different aspect of humanity as its focus.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. From the parent discipline to its specialized subfields._
      - Anthropology
      - Anthropology of development
      - Environmental anthropology
      - Linguistic anthropology
    - **Anthropology subfields that make claims about the human person, either as a gendered and sexual social being or as a biological organism, take the individual human as their unit of analysis.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure_
      - Anthropology of gender and sexuality
      - Biological (physical) anthropology
    - **Anthropology fields study human cultures and societies, each taking a different unit of cultural life as the subject of its claims.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds of anthropology distinguished by the cultural unit studied._
      - Medical anthropology
      - Social and cultural anthropology
      - Anthropology not elsewhere classified
  - **Demography fields make claims about populations, whether as aggregate demographic processes or as households and residual categories.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from population processes to the field and its household-level categories._
    - **Population process fields make claims about the aggregate demographic events of fertility, migration, mortality and overall population change.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds of demographic process studied at the population level._
      - Fertility
      - Migration
      - Mortality
      - Population trends and policies
    - **The demography field and its household-level and residual categories frame claims about populations and the family units that compose them.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds of demographic category by the unit of population or household._
      - Demography
      - Family and household studies
      - Demography not elsewhere classified
    - **Gender theory and method fields make claims about the conceptual frameworks and research approaches used to analyse gender.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds of theoretical and methodological approach within gender studies._
      - Gender studies
      - Feminist and queer theory
      - Feminist methodologies
      - Feminist theory
      - Intersectional studies
    - **Gender relations and identity fields make claims about relations between genders and about specific gendered or sexual groups.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds of gendered social group or relation studied._
      - Gender relations
      - Sexualities
      - Studies of men and masculinities
      - Transgender studies
      - Women's studies (incl. girls' studies)
  - **General social science categories take settings, persons, social groups, communities, whole societies, or the discipline itself as the unit their claims are about.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from settings and individuals through groups and communities to society-wide and broad residual categories._
    - **Individual and relational studies make claims about persons and their close relationships, bodies and life stages.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure: kinds of individual-level units._
      - Sociology of family and relationships
      - Sociology of the life course
      - Sociology of health
      - Sociology of gender
    - **Broad social science fields take the whole discipline or field of inquiry as the unit their claims are about.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the most general field umbrella to the more specific discipline of sociology._
      - Social sciences (General)
      - Anthropology, Education, Sociology, and Social Phenomena
      - Social Sciences
      - Sociology (General)
    - **Society and culture categories take an entire society, culture, or its customs as the unit their claims are about.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from general human culture and customs to specific or residual societal studies._
      - Anthropology
      - Manners and customs (General)
      - Studies of Asian society
      - Other human society not elsewhere classified
    - **Community and social condition categories take communities, classes, and social conditions or problems as the unit their claims are about.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from general social history and conditions to communities and then to social pathology and welfare._

    - **Persons and organized groups categories take people as individuals or as members of named or organized groups as the unit their claims are about.**

    - **Social group studies make claims about categories and collectivities of people defined by shared position, origin, belief or culture.**

      - Sociology of inequalities

      - Sociology of religion
      - Sociology of culture
    - **Settings and places studies make claims about the environments, localities and technical systems in which social life is situated.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure: kinds of settings the claim is about._
      - Environmental sociology
      - Rural sociology
      - Urban sociology and community studies
      - Sociology and social studies of science and technology
