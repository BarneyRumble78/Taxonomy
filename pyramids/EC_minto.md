# EC — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Economics studies how scarce resources are allocated among competing uses by people, firms, markets, institutions and governments.

**Complication.** The field's groups come from several published schemes that mix scale, method, sector and history, so they do not yet sit on one subject-matter dimension.

**Question.** How is everything in economics divided?

**Answer.** Economics divides by the scale at which an allocation is settled, from individual choice, to markets, to the mechanisms and institutions that govern them, to the aggregate economy, to welfare and public choice for the whole society.

## Governing thought

Economics divides by the scale at which an allocation is settled, from individual choice, to markets, to the mechanisms and institutions that govern them, to the aggregate economy, to welfare and public choice for the whole society.

## Key line

Group of scales of allocation. Basis: kinds. Dimension: the scale at which an allocation is settled. Order: degree. From one agent, to a market, to the whole society.

1. Choice is the scale at which allocation is settled by individual people, households and small groups through their own decisions under information, uncertainty and behavioral limits. `EC.K1`
2. Markets are the scale at which allocation is settled between many buyers and sellers, through production, prices, labor matching and the structure of industries. `EC.K2`
3. Mechanisms and institutions are the scale at which allocation is settled by the rules, organizations and systems that structure how markets and firms operate. `EC.K3`
4. The aggregate economy is the scale at which allocation is settled across a whole national or international economy, through prices, money, growth, trade and history. `EC.K4`
5. Welfare and public choice is the scale at which allocation is settled for the whole society, through collective decisions, government provision and the development of communities. `EC.K5`
