# EC — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Economics studies how scarce resources are allocated among competing uses.

**Complication.** The source schemes arrange the field by catalogue classes, mixing scales, sectors and methods.

**Question.** How is everything in economics divided?

**Answer.** Economics divides by the scale at which an allocation is settled, from one agent's choice, to markets, to the mechanisms and institutions that govern them, to the aggregate economy, to welfare and public choice for the whole society.

## Governing thought

Economics divides by the scale at which an allocation is settled, from one agent's choice, to markets, to the mechanisms and institutions that govern them, to the aggregate economy, to welfare and public choice for the whole society.

## Key line

Group of scales of allocation. Basis: kinds. Dimension: the scale at which an allocation is settled. Order: degree. From one agent, to a market, to the whole society.

1. Choice is the scale at which a single household, worker or decision-maker settles an allocation through preferences, information and behaviour. `EC.K1`
2. Markets are the scale at which many buyers and sellers settle an allocation through prices, competition and matching in firms, labour, finance and sectors. `EC.K2`
3. Mechanisms and institutions are the scale at which rules, organisations and systems settle an allocation across many agents and markets. `EC.K3`
4. The aggregate economy is the scale at which allocation is settled across a whole country, its borders and its long history. `EC.K4`
5. Welfare and public choice is the scale at which the whole society settles an allocation through governments, collective decisions, judgements of wellbeing and the justification of how allocation is studied. `EC.K5`
