# AR — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Arts and literature covers works made in many media, from text and stage to sound, image and screen.

**Complication.** Existing groupings mix media, national traditions and thesaurus facets, so the same aspect of a work appears under many headings.

**Question.** How is everything in arts and literature divided?

**Answer.** Arts and literature is divided by the feature of the work that a claim is about, running from the material object outward to judgment: medium and making, form, meaning, context and reception, and value.

## Governing thought

Arts and literature is divided by the feature of the work that a claim is about, running from the material object outward to judgment: medium and making, form, meaning, context and reception, and value.

## Key line

Group of aspects of the work. Basis: kinds. Dimension: the feature of the work that a claim is about. Order: structure. The aspects run from the material object outward to judgment of the work.

1. Medium and making is the aspect of the work concerned with the material or expressive medium in which it is made and the practice of making it. `AR.K1`
2. Form is the aspect of the work concerned with its own structure, features and attributes, such as genre, theoretical approach, design elements and physical properties. `AR.K2`
3. Meaning is the aspect of the work concerned with the concepts, representations and subjects it expresses or is about. `AR.K3`
4. Context and reception is the aspect of the work concerned with the place, tradition, language and cultural setting from which it comes and in which it is received. `AR.K4`
5. Value is the aspect of the work concerned with interpreting, evaluating and judging it within the broader scholarly and humanistic disciplines. `AR.K5`
