# AR — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Knowledge about arts and literature consists of claims about works: how they are made, how they look or are structured, what they mean, where and for whom they exist, and how they are judged.

**Complication.** The inherited groupings mix these features with library classes, residual buckets and container headings, so the same topic sits in several places and no one dimension organises the field.

**Question.** How should every topic in arts and literature be placed by the feature of the work it is about?

**Answer.** Arts and literature is divided by the feature of the work a claim is about, running outward from the material object to judgment: medium and making, form, meaning, context and reception, and value.

## Governing thought

Arts and literature is divided by the feature of the work a claim is about, running outward from the material object to judgment: medium and making, form, meaning, context and reception, and value.

## Key line

Group of features of the work. Basis: kinds. Dimension: what feature of the work the sentence is about. Order: structure. The pillars run from the material object outward to judgment of the work.

1. Medium and making is the feature of a work concerned with the material, technique and process by which it is produced. `AR.K1`
2. Form is the feature of a work concerned with its design, structure, genre and style as it appears to the audience. `AR.K2`
3. Meaning is the feature of a work concerned with what it represents, expresses and conveys through ideas and theory. `AR.K3`
4. Context and reception is the feature of a work concerned with the place, language, time, industry and audience in which it is produced and received. `AR.K4`
5. Value is the feature of a work concerned with how it is judged, studied historically and assigned standing within culture. `AR.K5`

### 1. Medium and making is the feature of a work concerned with the material, technique and process by which it is produced.

- **Medium and making in visual and material art concerns works produced by shaping physical materials with hand, tool or lens.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Subsections run from fine art media to craft, print and photographic processes._
  - **Medium and making in fine art covers painting, sculpture and drawing as the core physical media of visual art.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. The broad visual art field comes first, followed by its particular media._
    - Visual arts
    - Fine arts
    - Painting
    - Sculpture
    - Drawing. Design. Illustration
  - **Medium and making in craft, print and lens-based practice concerns works produced through reproducible or artisanal technical processes.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Handwork comes first, then printing, then photographic capture._
    - Crafts
    - Print media
    - Photography, video and lens-based practice
- **Medium and making in screen and digital media concerns works produced with cameras, computers and interactive technologies.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. The broad field comes first, then screen production, effects and interactive digital work._
  - Screen and digital media
  - Screen media
  - Visual effects
  - **Interactive and digital-art work is made with computing technology, such as games, animation, electronic art and interactive media.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure across the computing-based forms._
    - Computer gaming and animation
    - Digital and electronic media art
    - Interactive media
- **Medium and making in performance concerns works produced live through the bodies and actions of performers.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. The broad field comes first, then applied theatre and performance art._
  - Performing arts
  - Applied theatre
  - Performance art
- **Medium and making in writing concerns works produced by composing text for creative, digital, site-based or professional purposes.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. The broad field comes first, then creative, digital, site-based and professional writing._
  - Creative and professional writing
  - Creative writing (incl. scriptwriting)

### 2. Form is the feature of a work concerned with its design, structure, genre and style as it appears to the audience.

- **Form in visual works is the arrangement of design elements, colour and visible effects that an audience perceives.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Elements of design come first, then colour, then resulting conditions and effects._
  - Design Elements (hierarchy name)
  - Color (hierarchy name)
  - Conditions and Effects (hierarchy name)
- **Form in texts is the genre and type of writing that shapes how a written work appears to its readers.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Genre writing for general readers comes before writing for younger readers._
  - **Popular, genre, young adult and juvenile fiction are classified by the type of writing and the readership it is aimed at.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, not by degree._
    - Popular and genre literature
    - Young adult literature
    - Fiction and juvenile belles lettres
    - Stylistics and textual analysis
    - Print culture
  - **Literary studies defined by the type of text analyse how its kind and structure shape a written work for readers.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds are listed by approach without ranking._
    - Literary studies
    - Children's literature
    - Comparative and transnational literature
    - Digital literature
    - Literary theory

### 3. Meaning is the feature of a work concerned with what it represents, expresses and conveys through ideas and theory.

- **Meaning is explained by theoretical approaches that account for how visual art and cultural images produce significance.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Theory of visual art comes before theory of cultural representation._
  - Art theory
  - **Theory and representation studies examine how images, identities and meanings are produced and conceptualised in culture.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from visual cultures to general cultural theory._
    - Visual cultures
    - Art history, theory and criticism not elsewhere classified
    - Cultural theory
    - Culture, representation and identity
- **Meaning is carried by the ideas, themes and values that culture associates with works and that the works themselves convey.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. A single section of associated concepts; this pillar is completed by the theory section above._
  - Associated Concepts (hierarchy name)
  - Folklore

### 4. Context and reception is the feature of a work concerned with the place, language, time, industry and audience in which it is produced and received.

- **Context and reception by place and language groups literary works according to the region or tongue in which they were written.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Subsections move from Asia through Africa and the Middle East to Europe and the English-speaking world._
  - **Context and reception in East Asia covers literatures written in Chinese, Japanese and Korean.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Listed in a stable regional order._
    - Korean literature
    - Literature in Chinese
    - Literature in Japanese
  - **Context and reception in South and South-East Asia covers Indian, Indonesian and other South-East Asian literatures.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. South Asia comes before South-East Asia._
    - Indian literature
    - Indonesian literature
    - South-East Asian literature (excl. Indonesian)
  - **Context and reception in Africa and the Middle East covers literatures written across these neighbouring regions.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Africa comes before the Middle East._
    - African literature
    - Middle Eastern literature
  - **Context and reception in Europe covers classical, Central and Eastern European, French, German and Italian literatures.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Classical antiquity and the east come before the western European languages._
    - Latin and classical Greek literature
    - Central and Eastern European literature (incl. Russian)
    - Literature in French
    - Literature in German
    - Literature in Italian
  - **Context and reception in the English-speaking and Iberian-language world covers British, Irish, North American, Australasian and other literatures in English, Spanish and Portuguese.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. British and Irish literature comes first, then literatures that grew from it._
    - British and Irish literature
    - North American literature
    - Australian literature (excl. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander literature)
    - New Zealand literature (excl. Māori literature)
    - **Literary studies of works defined by their language of composition, covering Spanish and Portuguese literature as well as literatures written in English beyond the major national traditions.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure_
      - Other literatures in English
      - Literature in Spanish and Portuguese
- **Context and reception through cultural place and movement concerns how works arise from regions, migration and colonial encounter.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Place comes first, then movement of peoples and colonial legacies._
  - **Place-based cultural studies analyse culture in relation to particular regions, nations, environments and global flows.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the whole discipline to regional then global scales._
    - Cultural studies
    - Asian cultural studies
    - Cultural studies of nation and region
    - Environment and culture
    - Globalisation and culture
  - **Migration and postcolonial studies investigate cultural encounters, movement of peoples and the legacies of colonialism.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from migrant experience to intercultural contact to postcolonial critique._
    - Migrant cultural studies
    - Multicultural, intercultural and cross-cultural studies
    - Postcolonial studies
- **Context and reception through industry and audience concerns how works are produced, distributed, consumed and read.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Industry and policy come first, then media culture, then readership and material production of texts._
  - **Policy, industry and consumption studies look at how culture is organised, produced and used in economic and everyday life.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from policy to industry to everyday consumption._
    - Arts and cultural policy
    - Consumption and everyday life
    - Cultural and creative industries
    - Cultural studies of agriculture, food and wine
  - Screen and media culture
  - **Literary and cultural study fields defined by readership or material production analyse the circumstances in which texts are produced and received.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure_
    - Ecocriticism
    - Book history

### 5. Value is the feature of a work concerned with how it is judged, studied historically and assigned standing within culture.

- **Value in visual art is assigned through its history and through critical judgment of individual works.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. The combined field comes first, then criticism and history._
  - Art history, theory and criticism
  - Art criticism
  - Art history
- **Value in screen and performance is assigned through scholarly study and criticism of films, theatre and dance.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Cinema comes first, then theatre, then dance, following the medium pillar._
  - Cinema studies
  - Drama, theatre and performance studies
  - Dance and dance studies
- **Value in music is assigned through scholarly, historical and critical writing about musical works and traditions.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Musicology comes before the wider literature on music._
  - Musicology and ethnomusicology
  - Literature on music
