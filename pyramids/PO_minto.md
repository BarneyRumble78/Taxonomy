## Introduction

**Situation.** Politics studies how power is held, contested and used within and between political communities.

**Complication.** The many sources list power under theory, regime, movement, sector and region headings that mix dimensions.

**Question.** How is everything in politics divided by the aspect of power it concerns?

**Answer.** Politics is divided by the aspect of power the claim concerns, moving from the source of authority outward through institutions, political behaviour, and policy and administration to relations among states.

## Governing thought

Politics is divided by the aspect of power the claim concerns, moving from the source of authority outward through institutions, political behaviour, and policy and administration to relations among states.

## Key line

Group of aspects of power. Basis: kinds. Dimension: the aspect of power the claim concerns. Order: structure. The aspects run from the source of authority outward to its exercise across states.

1. Authority is the aspect of power concerned with the ideas, ideologies and conditions that found, justify and limit political rule. `PO.K1`
2. Institutions are the aspect of power concerned with the bodies, offices, regimes and organisations that hold and embody political authority. `PO.K2`
3. Political behaviour is the aspect of power concerned with how citizens and groups mobilise, participate, contest and resist in pursuit of power. `PO.K3`
4. Policy and administration is the aspect of power concerned with how public authority is exercised through governmental bodies in making and carrying out policy. `PO.K4`
5. Relations among states is the aspect of power concerned with how power is divided, projected and organised across territories, regions and countries. `PO.K5`
