# MS — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Psychology, neuroscience, sociology and anthropology all make claims about mind, brain and society, but at very different scales.

**Complication.** The sources group topics by library classes and professional fields, so the same unit of claim is scattered and mixed across many headings.

**Question.** How is everything in mind, brain and society divided?

**Answer.** Mind, brain and society are divided by the unit a claim is about, running from mental processes through nervous systems, persons and interactions to social structures.

## Governing thought

Mind, brain and society are divided by the unit a claim is about, running from mental processes through nervous systems, persons and interactions to social structures.

## Key line

Group of units of claim. Basis: kinds. Dimension: the unit the claim is about. Order: structure. Pillars run from the smallest unit that bears a mental claim to the largest pattern.

1. Mental processes are the unit of claim when psychology studies perception, thought, learning, emotion, awareness and the behaviour they produce. `MS.K1`
2. Nervous systems are the unit of claim when science studies the brain and nerves, their parts, functions and the methods used to measure them. `MS.K2`
3. Persons are the unit of claim when psychology studies the individual human being across life, personality, health, identity and the discipline itself. `MS.K3`
4. Interactions are the unit of claim when studies concern how people act together in groups, work, communication, law, play and professional service. `MS.K4`
5. Social structures are the unit of claim when studies concern institutions, cultures, populations, settings and society as a whole. `MS.K5`
