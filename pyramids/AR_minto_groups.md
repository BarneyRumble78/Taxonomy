# AR Minto groups

## AR.O.A1
- **Arts work fields can be classified by the main expressive medium that the work uses, whether text and stage, sound, or images and screens.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds are listed by expressive medium: text and stage, then sound, then images and screens._
  - **Written and live performance fields cover work whose main expressive medium is text or the embodied stage, whether creative, professional, or performed live.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from text-based writing fields to live performance._
    - **Creative-oriented writing covers work where the feature in focus is imaginative, digital, or place-based expression in text.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered as listed in ANZSRC code sequence._
      - Creative and professional writing
      - Creative writing (incl. scriptwriting)
      - Digital writing
      - Site-based writing
      - Creative and professional writing not elsewhere classified
    - **Professional and technical writing covers work where the feature in focus is informing audiences through journalism or specialised documentation.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered as listed in ANZSRC code sequence._
      - Professional writing and journalism practice
      - Technical writing
    - **Performing arts cover work where the feature in focus is live embodied performance in theatre, dance, and community settings.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered as listed in ANZSRC code sequence._
      - Performing arts
      - Applied theatre
      - Dance and dance studies
      - Drama, theatre and performance studies
      - Performing arts not elsewhere classified
  - **Music fields cover work whose main expressive medium is sound, including its study, its practice, and its general instruction.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from the study of music to its practice and general instruction._
    - **Music studies cover work where the feature in focus is understanding, teaching, and analysing music as a subject of inquiry.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered as listed in ANZSRC code sequence._
      - Music
      - Music cognition
      - Music education
      - Musicology and ethnomusicology
      - Music not elsewhere classified
    - **Music practice covers work where the feature in focus is the making, performing, and recording of music.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered as listed in ANZSRC code sequence._
      - Music composition and improvisation
      - Music performance
      - Music technology and recording
    - **Music work concerns the art of sound, including its instruction and study.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from music in general to its instruction and study._
      - Music
      - Instruction and study
  - **Visual and screen art fields cover work whose main expressive medium is images, objects, or screens, from hand-made crafts to digital and moving-image production.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from general and traditional visual art to print and photography, then screen and digital work._
    - **Moving-image and screen-based work covers film, screen media and visual effects as studied and produced for the screen.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the general screen field to specific screen forms._
      - Screen and digital media
      - Cinema studies
      - Screen media
      - Visual effects
      - Screen and digital media not elsewhere classified
    - **Interactive and digital-art work is made with computing technology, such as games, animation, electronic art and interactive media.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure across the computing-based forms._
      - Computer gaming and animation
      - Digital and electronic media art
      - Interactive media
    - **General visual arts categories are the broad umbrella fields for visual art practice, including residual and performance or lens-based forms.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the broad fields to their general subdivisions._
      - Visual arts
      - Visual arts
      - Performance art
      - Photography, video and lens-based practice
      - Visual arts not elsewhere classified
    - **Hand-made and traditional object-based art work covers crafts, fine arts, sculpture, drawing and painting made as physical artworks.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure across the traditional physical media._
      - Crafts
      - Fine arts
      - Sculpture
      - Drawing. Design. Illustration
      - Painting
    - **Print media, photography, and handicrafts are distinct kinds of visual and material art forms, each defined by the medium or technique used to make the work.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Members are arranged by structure of medium, from printed works to photographic images to handmade crafted objects, not ranked by degree._
      - Print media
      - Photography
      - Handicrafts. Arts and crafts

## AR.O.A3
- **Fields of literary study are divided by the feature of the work they address: its place or language of origin, a property of the text itself, or a general residual scope.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure_
  - **Literature fields defined by the region, nation, tradition or language community from which the writing comes.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from regional fields to language-based fields._
    - **This group covers the study of literature from Africa and Australia, defined by the region or nation of the writing.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds are listed by region without ranking._
      - African literature
      - Australian literature (excl. Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander literature)
      - British and Irish literature
      - Central and Eastern European literature (incl. Russian)
    - **This group covers the study of literature from South, Southeast and East Asia written in Indian, Indonesian, Korean, Chinese and Japanese traditions.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds are listed by region without ranking._
      - Indian literature
      - Indonesian literature
      - Korean literature
      - Literature in Chinese
      - Literature in Japanese
    - **This group covers the study of literature defined by the language of the writing, namely classical Latin and Greek, French, German and Italian.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds are listed by language without ranking._
      - Latin and classical Greek literature
      - Literature in French
      - Literature in German
      - Literature in Italian
    - **Literatures of the Spanish, Portuguese, Middle Eastern, Asian, European, English-language and other regions are classified by the place or language community the work comes from.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure of the regional scheme, not by degree._
      - New Zealand literature (excl. Māori literature)
      - French literature - Italian literature - Spanish literature - Portuguese
      - English literature
      - American literature
      - German literature - Dutch literature - Flemish literature since 1830
    - **Each of these fields studies the literature of a particular world region or tradition, such as Asian, Middle Eastern, North American or European writing, that has no field of its own.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure_
      - Other Asian literature (excl. South-East Asian)
      - Middle Eastern literature
      - South-East Asian literature (excl. Indonesian)
      - North American literature
      - Other European literature
  - **Literary study fields defined by a feature of the text itself, such as theoretical approach, medium, genre, readership or material production.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from approach-based fields to genre-based fields._
    - **This group covers literary studies defined by theoretical approach, medium or type of text rather than by national tradition.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds are listed by approach without ranking._
      - Literary studies
      - Children's literature
      - Comparative and transnational literature
      - Digital literature
      - Literary theory
    - **Each of these literary and cultural study fields is defined by a particular feature of a work that its analysis addresses, such as its environmental concerns or its physical production and circulation as a book.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure_
      - Ecocriticism
      - Book history
    - **Popular, genre, young adult and juvenile fiction are classified by the type of writing and the readership it is aimed at.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, not by degree._
      - Popular and genre literature
      - Young adult literature
      - Fiction and juvenile belles lettres
      - Stylistics and textual analysis
      - Print culture
  - **Literary study fields covering general or residual scope, including broad unclassified work and English-language and Iberian literatures outside the main national traditions.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from the general field to the residual language field._
    - **General and unclassified literary studies cover work that is not tied to a particular region, genre or feature of the text.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, not by degree._
      - Literary studies not elsewhere classified
      - Literature (General)
    - **Literary studies of works defined by their language of composition, covering Spanish and Portuguese literature as well as literatures written in English beyond the major national traditions.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure_
      - Other literatures in English
      - Literature in Spanish and Portuguese

## AR.O.A4
- **Scholarly approaches to art study the interpretive and conceptual features of a work, namely its historical meaning, critical evaluation and cultural representation.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure_
  - **Art history, theory and criticism is the broad field of scholarship that interprets, evaluates and historically situates works of art.**
    _basis: kinds, order: degree. Broadest field in this group._
    - **Art history, theory and criticism is the field of scholarship that interprets, evaluates and historically situates works of art.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the whole field to its main sub-fields._
      - Art history, theory and criticism
      - Art criticism
      - Art history
      - Art theory
    - **Migration and postcolonial studies investigate cultural encounters, movement of peoples and the legacies of colonialism.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from migrant experience to intercultural contact to postcolonial critique._
      - Migrant cultural studies
      - Multicultural, intercultural and cross-cultural studies
      - Postcolonial studies
    - **Media-oriented cultural studies fields take screen media and general cultural analysis as the subject of the work.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the specific media field to the general residual category._
      - Screen and media culture
      - Cultural studies not elsewhere classified
    - **Traditional and musical expression literature covers the folk practices and musical works that a text is about.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from folklore to writing about music._
      - Folklore
      - Literature on music
    - **Humanities-field concepts name the broad scholarly and artistic disciplines that frame a work as belonging to the humanities or arts.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the general humanities headings to the arts and music literature._
      - Humanities
      - Humanities
      - Arts in general
  - **Theory and representation studies examine how images, identities and meanings are produced and conceptualised in culture.**
    _basis: kinds, order: degree. Narrower approach focused on meaning and representation._
    - **Theory and representation studies examine how images, identities and meanings are produced and conceptualised in culture.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from visual cultures to general cultural theory._
      - Visual cultures
      - Art history, theory and criticism not elsewhere classified
      - Cultural theory
      - Culture, representation and identity
    - **Place-based cultural studies analyse culture in relation to particular regions, nations, environments and global flows.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the whole discipline to regional then global scales._
      - Cultural studies
      - Asian cultural studies
      - Cultural studies of nation and region
      - Environment and culture
      - Globalisation and culture
    - **Policy, industry and consumption studies look at how culture is organised, produced and used in economic and everyday life.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from policy to industry to everyday consumption._
      - Arts and cultural policy
      - Consumption and everyday life
      - Cultural and creative industries
      - Cultural studies of agriculture, food and wine
