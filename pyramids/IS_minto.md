# IS — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Information science studies records as they are made, described, ordered, found, used and kept by people and institutions.

**Complication.** The inherited groupings mix stages, repeat the same topics from different sources and include residual and heading-only items, so no one can tell which stage a topic belongs to.

**Question.** How can every topic in information science be placed under exactly one stage in the life of a record?

**Answer.** Information science is divided by the stage in the life of a record, from creation to keeping: creation and publication, description, arrangement, retrieval and use, and preservation and governance.

## Governing thought

Information science is divided by the stage in the life of a record, from creation to keeping: creation and publication, description, arrangement, retrieval and use, and preservation and governance.

## Key line

Group of stages. Basis: cause_effect. Dimension: the stage in the life of a record. Order: time. The stages run in the order a record passes through them, from its creation to its long-term keeping.

1. Creation and publication is the stage in which records come into being, are produced and are issued to their audiences. `IS.K1`
2. Description is the stage in which a record is named, listed and indexed so that its content and subject are represented. `IS.K2`
3. Arrangement is the stage in which described records are ordered into classes by classification systems, theory and control structures. `IS.K3`
4. Retrieval and use is the stage in which arranged records are stored, searched for, found and used by their readers. `IS.K4`
5. Preservation and governance is the final stage in which records are kept, protected and managed over the long term. `IS.K5`
