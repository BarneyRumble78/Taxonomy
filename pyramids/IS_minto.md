# IS — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Records of every kind are made, issued, described, found, used and kept by libraries, archives and information services.

**Complication.** Sources describe the same stages under many different names and mix catalogue classes with subject matter, so topics sit in overlapping groups.

**Question.** How is everything in information science divided?

**Answer.** Information science is divided by the stage in the life of a record into creation, production and publication, description and organization, retrieval and use, and preservation and governance.

## Governing thought

Information science is divided by the stage in the life of a record into creation, production and publication, description and organization, retrieval and use, and preservation and governance.

## Key line

Group of stages. Basis: cause_effect. Dimension: the stage in the life of a record. Order: time. The stages run in the order a record passes through them, from its creation to its long-term keeping.

1. Creation is the first stage of a record's life, in which information is generated, captured and first recorded by its originators. `IS.K1`
2. Production and publication is the stage in which created records are manufactured, issued and distributed to their audiences. `IS.K2`
3. Description and organization is the stage in which records are analysed, described, classified and structured so that they can be found. `IS.K3`
4. Retrieval and use is the stage in which records are searched for, found, consulted and turned into knowledge by their users. `IS.K4`
5. Preservation and governance is the final stage, in which records are held, protected, managed and regulated over the long term. `IS.K5`
