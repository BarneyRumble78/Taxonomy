# BU — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Firms record, fund, run, promote and trade, and knowledge about business has grown up around each of these practices separately.

**Complication.** The material arrives mixed from research fields and industry classes, so the same practice appears under several different headings and levels.

**Question.** How is everything in business divided?

**Answer.** Business divides by the practice of the firm, running from the record outward to customers and borders, into accounts, finance, operations and organisation, marketing, and trade and tax.

## Governing thought

Business divides by the practice of the firm, running from the record outward to customers and borders, into accounts, finance, operations and organisation, marketing, and trade and tax.

## Key line

Group of practices of the firm. Basis: kinds. Dimension: the practice of the firm. Order: structure. The practices run from the firm's own record, through its funding and internal running, outward to its customers and then its trading and taxation across markets and borders.

1. Accounts is the practice of the firm in which financial information is recorded, reported, audited and made accountable. `BU.K1`
2. Finance is the practice of the firm in which funds, credit, investment and risk are raised, held, exchanged and insured. `BU.K2`
3. Operations and organisation is the practice of the firm in which goods and services are produced, delivered and managed. `BU.K3`
4. Marketing is the practice of the firm in which offerings are understood, priced, promoted and delivered to customers, including tourism and hospitality. `BU.K4`
5. Trade and tax is the practice of the firm in which goods are bought and resold across markets and borders, with the commercial dealings that carry the firm's tax and trading obligations. `BU.K5`
