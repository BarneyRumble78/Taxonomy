# EC Minto groups

## EC.O.A1
- **The scale at which an allocation is settled organizes economic work into analysis by scale, disciplinary framing, and general supporting material.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from scale-specific analysis to framing to general material._
  - **Scale-based allocation analysis covers work that places the settling of allocation at the firm and market, the whole economy or collective, or the conditions of individual choice.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from firm and market, to the whole economy, to the conditions of individual choice._
    - **Allocation is settled at the scale of the firm and the market, through production, the distribution of income, and the pricing and design of markets.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the firm to the market._
      - Production and Organizations
      - Distribution
      - Market Structure, Pricing, and Design
    - **Allocation is settled at the scale of the whole economy or collective, through general equilibrium, welfare assessment, and collective decision-making.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from economy-wide equilibrium to collective choice._
      - General Equilibrium and Disequilibrium
      - Welfare Economics
      - Analysis of Collective Decision-Making
    - **Allocation is shaped by the conditions under which individual agents choose, such as information, uncertainty, behavioral limits, and general theory.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from information to behavior to general theory._
      - Information, Knowledge, and Uncertainty
      - Micro-Based Behavioral Economics
      - Economic theory. Demography
  - **Disciplinary and historical framing covers formal theory, the history of ideas and methodology that frame how allocation is studied.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from formal theory, to history of thought, to methodology and heterodox critique._
    - **Aggregate and formal theory covers allocation analysed at the level of the whole economy or through general mathematical models.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the parent field to its subfields._
      - Economic theory
      - Macroeconomic theory
      - Mathematical economics
      - Economic theory not elsewhere classified
    - **Historical thought covers how ideas about allocation developed across successive periods and through particular thinkers.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the general category to period and individual subdivisions._
      - History of economic thought
      - History of Economic Thought through 1925
      - History of Economic Thought since 1925
      - History of Economic Thought:  Individuals
    - **Methodology and heterodox approaches cover the general framing of the discipline and the ways economists justify or challenge its methods.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the general category to specific approaches._
      - History of Economic Thought, Methodology, and Heterodox Approaches
      - General
      - Economic Methodology
      - Current Heterodox Approaches
  - **General and supporting material covers individual-scale economics, broad overviews, reference content and unclassified items that are not tied to one allocation scale.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from individual-scale economics, to general economics, to reference material, to unclassified items._
    - **Individual-scale economics covers allocation settled by single decision-makers such as households, firms and markets.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the parent field to its subfields._
      - Microeconomic theory
      - Microeconomics
      - General
      - Household Behavior and Family Economics
    - **General economics and teaching covers broad overviews, education in economics and collected works not tied to one allocation scale.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the general category to its subdivisions._
      - General Economics and Teaching
      - General Economics
      - Economic Education and Teaching of Economics
      - Collective Works
    - **Reference and data material is supporting content that presents tables, introductions, excerpts, or related disciplines rather than allocation theory.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the general category to specific reference items._
      - Miscellaneous Categories
      - Data: Tables and Charts
      - Introductory Material
      - Excerpts
      - Related Disciplines
    - **Unclassified commentary and other items are miscellaneous works such as reviews, dissertations, further reading, and anonymous discussions that fit no specific subject.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from reviews and dissertations to residual items._
      - Book Reviews (unclassified)
      - Dissertations (unclassified)
      - Further Reading (unclassified)
      - No Author General Discussions
      - Other

## EC.O.A2
- **Allocation can be settled at five different scales, from individual choice through sectors, places and public authorities to the whole economy.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally by the scale at which allocation is settled, running from individuals and households up through sectors, places and governments to the whole economy._
  - **Allocation settled by individuals, households and workers through personal choices and labor market matching and bargaining.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from individual choice to group-level worker movement._
    - **Fields where allocation is settled by individual people and small groups through their choices, learning and work decisions.**
      _basis: kinds, order: degree. Smallest scale: individual and small-group decisions._
      - Behavioural economics
      - Experimental economics
      - Economics of education
      - Labour economics
    - **Allocation settled at the individual and household scale, where persons decide on saving, behavior and family composition.**
      _basis: kinds, order: degree. Smallest scale: individual and household._
      - Behavioral Finance
      - Household Finance
      - Demographic Economics
      - Financial Institutions and Services
      - Corporate Finance and Governance
    - **Allocation settled in the labor market through the matching of workers and employers on supply, demand, pay and occupation.**
      _basis: kinds, order: degree. Third scale: market-level exchange of labor._
      - Demand and Supply of Labor
      - Wages, Compensation, and Labor Costs
      - Particular Labor Markets
      - Labor Discrimination
    - **Allocation settled through collective bargaining and movement of workers across jobs, places and borders at the group scale.**
      _basis: kinds, order: degree. Fourth scale: collective and group-level labor arrangements._
      - Labor&ndash;Management Relations, Trade Unions, and Collective Bargaining
      - Mobility, Unemployment, Vacancies, and Immigrant Workers
  - **Allocation settled within industries and sectors through the behaviour, structure and regulation of firms and providers.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the general industry category to firm-level and sector-level detail._
    - **Fields where allocation is settled within particular industries and sectors through the behaviour of firms and providers.**
      _basis: kinds, order: degree. Second scale: firm and sector level, above the individual._
      - Agricultural economics
      - Industry economics and industrial organisation
      - Tourism economics
      - Transport economics
      - Health economics
    - **Broad economic and sectoral fields settle the allocation of land, labor, industry and general applied economic activity at the aggregate scale.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Aggregate, economy-wide scale of allocation._
      - Applied economics not elsewhere classified
      - Industries. Land use. Labor
    - **Specific markets and networks settle allocation at a narrower scale, through financial market trading and through transportation and communications systems.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Market- and network-level scale of allocation._
      - General Financial Markets
      - Transportation and communications
    - **Market structure, firm objectives, nonprofit forms, and antitrust describe allocation settled by firms' strategies, ownership, and competition enforcement.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from firm strategy to competition policy._
      - Market Structure, Firm Strategy, and Market Performance
      - Firm Objectives, Organization, and Behavior
      - Nonprofit Organizations and Public Enterprise
      - Antitrust Issues and Policies
    - **Regulation and industry studies of manufacturing, primary products, and services describe allocation settled through public rules and at the sector level.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from regulation to sector studies._
      - Regulation and Industrial Policy
      - Industry Studies: Manufacturing
      - Industry Studies: Primary Products and Construction
      - Industry Studies: Services
  - **Allocation settled by the use of land, natural resources, networks and location within places and regions.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from land-based and network production to resource stocks and location choices._
    - **Fields where allocation is settled by the use of land, natural resources and location within places and regions.**
      _basis: kinds, order: degree. Third scale: place and resource level, spanning localities and regions._
      - Environment and resource economics
      - Urban and regional economics
    - **Utilities and the general, agricultural, and renewable resource topics describe allocation settled in network industries and land-based production.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from utilities to resource topics._
      - Industry Studies: Transportation and Utilities
      - General
      - Agriculture
      - Renewable Resources and Conservation
    - **Nonrenewable resources, energy, the environment, and general regional economics describe allocation settled at the scale of resource stocks, ecosystems, and places.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from resources to environment to regional._
      - Nonrenewable Resources and Conservation
      - Energy
      - Environmental Economics
      - General
    - **This group covers allocations settled by individual households, firms, and travelers choosing where to live, locate, and move.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure: household, then firm and property market, then transport network._
      - Household Analysis
      - Real Estate Markets, Spatial Production Analysis, and Firm Location
      - Transportation Economics
  - **Allocation settled collectively by governments and regional authorities through political choice, public provision and taxation.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from national government to regional government scale._
    - **Fields where allocation is settled collectively by governments through political choice, public provision, taxation and distributional judgement.**
      _basis: kinds, order: degree. Fourth scale: collective and state-level settlement._
      - Public economics - public choice
      - Public economics - publicly provided goods
      - Public economics - taxation and revenue
      - Welfare economics
    - **This group covers allocations settled at the aggregate scale of whole regions and their governments, not by single decision units.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure: region as a whole, then its government._
      - General Regional Economics
      - Regional Government Analysis
  - **Allocation settled at the scale of the whole economy, across borders and history, or classified under broad general headings.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the economy-wide field to national and international scale and general headings._
    - **Fields where allocation is settled at the level of the whole economy, across borders, financial systems and long historical spans, together with the general field of applied economics.**
      _basis: kinds, order: degree. Largest scale: economy-wide and international settlement._
      - Macroeconomics (incl. monetary and fiscal theory)
      - International economics
      - Financial economics
      - Economic history
      - Applied economics
    - **Allocation settled at the economy-wide, national or international scale, or classified under broad general headings.**
      _basis: kinds, order: degree. Largest scale: economy-wide and general._
      - Financial Economics
      - General
      - Labor and Demographic Economics
      - General
      - Labor Standards: National and International
    - **Broad field headings and the general industrial organization entry define the widest scale at which allocation questions are posed.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from the field headings to the general entry._
      - Industrial Organization
      - Agricultural and Natural Resource Economics &bull; Environmental and Ecological Economics
      - Urban, Rural, Regional, Real Estate, and Transportation Economics
      - General

## EC.O.A3
- **The scale at which an allocation is settled ranges from the single firm up to the whole political economy and its legal and institutional frameworks.**
  _basis: kinds, order: degree. Ranked from the narrowest scale of settlement, inside the firm and its legal rules, to the broadest, the whole economic system._
  - **Allocation is settled by substantive legal rules that assign rights and duties across the basic, regulatory and other areas of law.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure of the classification, from the general field to its substantive areas._
    - Law and Economics
    - General
    - Basic Areas of Law
    - Regulation and Business Law
    - Other Substantive Areas of Law
  - **Allocation is settled through legal procedure and the legal system, and at the general and administrative level of the business organization.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure of the classification, from procedure to the general business field and its administration._
    - Legal Procedure, the Legal System, and Illegal Behavior
    - Business Administration and Business Economics &bull; Marketing &bull; Accounting &bull; Personnel Economics
    - General
    - Business Administration
  - **Allocation is settled inside the firm through business economics, marketing, accounting and the management of personnel.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure of the classification, from business economics to personnel._
    - Business Economics
    - Marketing and Advertising
    - Accounting and Auditing
    - Personnel Economics
  - **Allocation is settled at the level of the whole political economy, in general terms and in capitalist and socialist or transition economies.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure of the classification, from the general field to specific economy types._
    - Political Economy and Comparative Economic Systems
    - General
    - Capitalist Economies
    - Socialist and Transition Economies
  - **Allocation is settled by the institutions of an economic system, whether socialist, other, or compared across systems.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure of the classification, from socialist institutions to comparison of systems._
    - Socialist Institutions and Their Transitions
    - Other Economic Systems
    - Comparative Economic Systems

## EC.O.A4
- **Economics categories are distinguished by the scale at which an allocation or estimation is settled, ranging from a single relationship up to whole nations.**
  _basis: kinds, order: degree. Ranked by scale of settlement, from a single equation or experiment, through systems and economy-wide aggregates, to relations across whole nations._
  - **Methods and econometrics categories settle allocation questions at scales running from the whole discipline down to a single equation, a system, or an experiment.**
    _basis: kinds, order: degree. Ranked from broadest scale (umbrella fields) to narrowest (single equations, strategic systems, and designed trials)._
    - **Development studies categories settled at the community and household scale address poverty and local rural and urban development.**
      _basis: kinds, order: degree. Ranked from household wellbeing to rural communities to urban communities._
      - Poverty, inclusivity and wellbeing
      - Rural community development
      - Urban community development
      - Development studies
    - **General and economy-wide historical categories treat allocation as settled at the aggregate scale of whole economies, their conditions, growth, and fluctuations.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from the general history category to macroeconomic history._
      - Economic history and conditions
      - Economic History
      - General
      - Macroeconomics and Monetary Economics &bull; Industrial Structure &bull; Growth &bull; Fluctuations
    - **Historical categories on institutions treat allocation as settled at the scale of financial systems, society and households, and the state and law.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from financial institutions to social and then governmental institutions._
      - Financial Markets and Institutions
      - Labor and Consumers, Demography, Education, Health, Welfare, Income, Wealth, Religion, and Philanthropy
      - Government, War, Law, International Relations, and Regulation
    - **Historical categories on sectors, firms, and places treat allocation as settled at the scale of particular industries, businesses, and localities.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from resource sectors to manufacturing, services, firms, and regions._
      - Agriculture, Natural Resources, Environment, and Extractive Industries
      - Manufacturing and Construction
      - Transport, Trade, Energy, Technology, and Other Services
      - Micro-Business History
      - Regional and Urban History
    - **Development categories treat allocation as settled through the long-run growth of economies and the planning and policy that guide it.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from the general field to development and then planning and policy._
      - Economic Development, Innovation, Technological Change, and Growth
      - Economic Development
      - Development Planning and Policy
  - **Methods and fields of economics are distinguished by the scale at which an allocation or estimation is settled, from a single relationship or dataset up to interacting systems and whole nations.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure_
    - **Multi-equation, mathematical, strategic and experimental methods settle allocations at the scale of interacting systems, players, or designed trials.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from simultaneous systems to programming, games and experiments._
      - Multiple or Simultaneous Equation Models &bull; Multiple Variables
      - Mathematical Methods &bull; Programming Models &bull; Mathematical and Simulation Modeling
      - Game Theory and Bargaining Theory
      - Design of Experiments
    - **Single-equation and special-topic methods settle estimation at the scale of one relationship, model specification, or dataset.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from single equations to modeling, special topics and data construction._
      - Single Equation Models &bull; Single Variables
      - Econometric and Statistical Methods:  Special Topics
      - Econometric Modeling
      - Data Collection and Data Estimation Methodology &bull; Computer Programs
    - **Behavioral macro and international economics cover allocations settled by aggregate psychology and across national borders through trade.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from behavioral macro to the international field and trade._
      - Macro-Based Behavioral Economics
      - International Economics
      - General
      - Trade
    - **Data-structure categories in econometrics settle analysis at the scale of how observations are arranged across units and time.**
      _basis: kinds, order: degree. Ranked from a single time point across units, to one unit over time, to many units over time._
      - Cross-sectional analysis
      - Time-series analysis
      - Panel data analysis
      - Socio-economic development
    - **Development studies categories settled at the national and international scale address aid, conflict, labour flows and political structures.**
      _basis: kinds, order: degree. Ranked from international cooperation to crises and migration to national political economy._
      - Development cooperation
      - Humanitarian disasters, conflict and peacebuilding
      - Labour, migration and development
      - Political economy and social change
  - **Economics topic areas are distinguished by the scale at which an allocation is settled, from discipline-wide headings to the whole economy's aggregates, its price and policy mechanisms, and development across whole countries.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure_
    - **Prices, money and policy cover allocations settled economy-wide through price movements, interest rates, central banks, and government macro policy.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from prices and cycles to money, central banking and fiscal policy._
      - Prices, Business Fluctuations, and Cycles
      - Money and Interest Rates
      - Monetary Policy, Central Banking, and the Supply of Money and Credit
      - Macroeconomic Policy, Macroeconomic Aspects of Public Finance, and General Outlook
    - **Macroeconomic overview and aggregate behavior covers how the whole economy's consumption, saving, production, and labor are settled in total.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the field itself to general models and real aggregates._
      - Macroeconomics and Monetary Economics
      - General
      - General Aggregative Models
      - Consumption, Saving, Production, Investment, Labor Markets, and Informal Economy
    - **Fields of development and growth economics distinguished by the scale at which resource allocation is settled, from innovation activity to aggregate productivity to whole-country experience.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from the narrowest scale (innovation and technology) to the broadest (economywide country studies)._
      - Innovation &bull; Research and Development &bull; Technological Change &bull; Intellectual Property Rights
      - Economic Growth and Aggregate Productivity
      - Economywide Country Studies
    - **Top-level umbrella fields and general method headings settle quantitative questions at the broadest, discipline-wide scale.**
      _basis: kinds, order: degree. Ranked from the broadest heading (Mathematical and Quantitative Methods) to the narrower general headings beneath it._
      - Econometrics
      - Mathematical and Quantitative Methods
      - General
      - Econometric and Statistical Methods and Methodology:  General
  - **Fields of economics are distinguished by the scale at which an allocation is settled, from the general level of techniques and models to the level of relations across national borders.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure_
    - **Methods-oriented econometric categories settle estimation questions at the level of general techniques and models rather than a particular dataset.**
      _basis: kinds, order: degree. Ranked from general statistical methods to model-building and forecasting to residual catch-all categories._
      - Econometric and statistical methods
      - Economic models and forecasting
      - Econometrics not elsewhere classified
      - Development studies not elsewhere classified
    - **International economics fields treat allocation as settled across national borders through factor movements, finance, macro linkages, political relations, and globalization.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from factor flows to finance, macro, political, and global impacts._
      - International Factor Movements and International Business

## EC.O.A5
- **The scale at which an allocation is settled divides into aggregate settlement at the level of the whole economy or national government and disaggregated settlement at local, sectoral, household, individual and community levels.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure_
  - **Aggregate settlement is allocation decided at the scale of the whole economy or the national government, together with the umbrella headings that span the field.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the whole economy to national government to general headings._
    - **Economy-wide settlement covers the whole-system and alternative-paradigm areas of economics that study how an entire economy allocates resources.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Largest scale: the whole economic system._
      - Other economics
      - Comparative economic systems
      - Ecological economics
      - Heterodox economics
      - Other economics not elsewhere classified
    - **Whole-government settlement covers allocations decided at the level of the national government through its scope, revenue, spending and budget.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. National scale, below the whole economy._
      - Structure and Scope of Government
      - Taxation, Subsidies, and Revenue
      - National Government Expenditures and Related Policies
      - National Budget, Deficit, and Debt
      - Public finance
    - **General and umbrella headings are top-level categories that span the field without fixing a particular scale of settlement.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Scale-neutral catch-all headings placed last._
      - Public Economics
      - General
      - Health, Education, and Welfare
      - General
  - **Disaggregated settlement is allocation decided below the national aggregate, in local and sectoral arrangements, particular industries, households, individuals and social communities.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from sub-national and sectoral arrangements to individuals, communities and industries._
    - **Sub-national and sectoral settlement covers allocations decided through specific public goods, local and intergovernmental arrangements and miscellaneous public issues.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Intermediate scale between national government and individuals._
      - Publicly Provided Goods
      - State and Local Government &bull; Intergovernmental Relations
      - Miscellaneous Issues
    - **Individual and household settlement covers allocations settled through the behavior of agents and their health and education outcomes.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Smallest scale: the individual agent._
      - Fiscal Policies and Behavior of Economic Agents
      - Health
      - Education and Research Institutions
    - **Allocations in this group are settled at the level of households, individuals and social communities, through welfare outcomes and cultural or social norms.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from the household and individual scale to the broader cultural and community scale._
      - Welfare, Well-Being, and Poverty
      - Cultural Economics &bull; Economic Sociology &bull; Economic Anthropology
    - **Allocations in this group are settled at the level of particular industries and sector markets, such as sports and tourism, plus the general residual category.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from the general residual category to specific sector markets._
      - Other Special Topics
      - General
      - Sports Economics
      - Tourism Economics
