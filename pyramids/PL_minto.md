# PL — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Philosophy asks questions about reality, knowledge, meaning, value and inquiry, and has been organised by many schemes.

**Complication.** Existing groupings mix subject matter with periods, regions, school-level teaching and catch-all headings, so related questions are scattered.

**Question.** How is everything in philosophy divided by the kind of question it asks?

**Answer.** Philosophy is divided by what its questions are about: being, knowledge and mind, meaning and inference, value, and the standing of the sciences, mathematics and philosophy itself.

## Governing thought

Philosophy is divided by what its questions are about: being, knowledge and mind, meaning and inference, value, and the standing of the sciences, mathematics and philosophy itself.

## Key line

Group of claim-types. Basis: kinds. Dimension: what the question is about. Order: structure. The pillars run from being, to knowing, to saying, to valuing, to the standing of the sciences and of inquiry itself.

1. Being is the claim-type concerned with what exists, including fundamental reality, religious questions, action and the place of consciousness in nature. `PL.K1`
2. Knowledge and mind is the claim-type concerned with how we know, what the mind is, and how minds malfunction. `PL.K2`
3. Meaning and inference is the claim-type concerned with valid reasoning and how meaning is produced and understood in language and texts. `PL.K3`
4. Value is the claim-type concerned with what is good, right, beautiful and just, in private life, society and the professions. `PL.K4`
5. The standing of the sciences, mathematics and philosophy is the claim-type concerned with the foundations, methods and history of inquiry, including philosophy's own. `PL.K5`
