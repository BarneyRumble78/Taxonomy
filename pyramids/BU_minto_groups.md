# BU Minto groups

## BU.O.A1
- **The practice of the firm in accounting is made up of its core methods, its context-specific adaptations, and the oversight that assures accountability.**
  _basis: parts, order: structure. Parts are ordered from the core practices to their adaptations and then to oversight and general classification._
  - **Core accounting practices are the standards-based and internal methods a firm uses to record, report, and manage its financial information.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from standards and theory through external reporting to internal management use._
    - Accounting theory and standards
    - Financial accounting
    - Management accounting
    - Taxation accounting
  - **Context-specific accounting adapts firm accounting to particular settings such as cross-border operations, non-profit organisations, and sustainability concerns.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally by the organisational or environmental context being served._
    - International accounting
    - Not-for-profit accounting and accountability
    - Sustainability accounting and reporting
  - **Oversight and general classification covers the assurance of accountability in firms along with the umbrella field and its unclassified remainder.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from the umbrella field to its oversight function and residual category._
    - Accounting, auditing and accountability
    - Auditing and accountability
    - Accounting, auditing and accountability not elsewhere classified

      - Financial econometrics
    - **Research fields applying finance to specific purposes such as the environment, households, investment risk and not-for-profit organisations.**

      - Investment and risk management
