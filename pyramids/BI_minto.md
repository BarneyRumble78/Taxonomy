# BI — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Biological claims are made about living things at many scales, from nucleic acid sequences to whole communities.

**Complication.** The existing groups mix subject matter with source schemes, and several are residual headings or container labels.

**Question.** How is everything in biology divided?

**Answer.** Biology is divided by the level or process a claim concerns, running from the copied sequence to the interacting community: heritable information, the cell, the organism, descent, and ecological interaction.

## Governing thought

Biology is divided by the level or process a claim concerns, running from the copied sequence to the interacting community: heritable information, the cell, the organism, descent, and ecological interaction.

## Key line

Group of levels or processes. Basis: kinds. Dimension: the level or process the claim is about. Order: structure. The pillars run from the copied sequence up to the interacting community.

1. Heritable information is the level at which claims concern copied sequences, genes, genomes and the chemistry of nucleic acids, together with the computational analysis of them. `BI.K1`
2. The cell is the level at which claims concern biomolecules, cellular processes, regulatory factors, biological materials and their industrial and environmental uses. `BI.K2`
3. The organism is the level at which claims concern whole living bodies, their kinds, their structures and their functions. `BI.K3`
4. Descent is the process by which lineages are classified, adapt, interact with other species and diversify or vanish over evolutionary time. `BI.K4`
5. Ecological interaction is the level at which claims concern organisms living together in populations, communities and habitats, including their past. `BI.K5`
