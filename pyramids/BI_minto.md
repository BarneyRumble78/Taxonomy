# BI — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Biological knowledge spans molecules, cells, whole organisms, lineages and ecosystems.

**Complication.** Source schemes mix anatomy, chemistry, methods and taxa, so topics sit in scattered places.

**Question.** How is everything in biology divided?

**Answer.** Biological claims are divided by the level or process they concern, running from the copied sequence to the interacting community: heritable information, the cell, the organism, descent, and ecological interaction.

## Governing thought

Biological claims are divided by the level or process they concern, running from the copied sequence to the interacting community: heritable information, the cell, the organism, descent, and ecological interaction.

## Key line

Group of levels or processes. Basis: kinds. Dimension: the level or process the claim is about. Order: structure. The pillars run from the copied sequence through the cell and the organism to descent and the interacting community.

1. Heritable information is the level at which claims concern copied sequences, genes, genomes and the molecules that carry them. `BI.K1`
2. The cell is the level at which claims concern biomolecules, cellular processes, signals and the materials cells make. `BI.K2`
3. The organism is the level at which claims concern the body structures, physiology, behaviour and kinds of whole living individuals. `BI.K3`
4. Descent is the process by which claims concern how lineages are related, adapt, split and vanish over evolutionary time. `BI.K4`
5. Ecological interaction is the level at which claims concern organisms relating to each other, to habitats and to the wider environment. `BI.K5`
