# HI — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** History studies the human past through the evidence it left and the accounts later people have made of it.

**Complication.** The field's records mix evidence, periods, places, themes and heritage practice under catalogue headings that hide what each one asserts about the past.

**Question.** How is everything in history divided by what it asserts about the human past?

**Answer.** History's objects divide by what a sentence asserts about the human past, running from the single fact to its meaning: occurrence, time, cause, change and continuity, and meaning and memory.

## Governing thought

History's objects divide by what a sentence asserts about the human past, running from the single fact to its meaning: occurrence, time, cause, change and continuity, and meaning and memory.

## Key line

Group of assertions about the past. Basis: cause_effect. Dimension: what the sentence asserts about the human past. Order: time. The pillars run from the single established fact to the meaning later given to it.

1. Occurrence is the assertion that something happened, established from the material, written and personal evidence the past left behind. `HI.K1`
2. Time is the assertion that events belong to a particular moment, ordered by calendars and divided into eras. `HI.K2`
3. Cause is the assertion that events happened because of political, social, imperial or other human forces that shaped them. `HI.K3`
4. Change and continuity is the assertion that peoples, states and the human past as a whole persisted or were transformed over time, within places and across borders. `HI.K4`
5. Meaning and memory is the assertion that the past matters through how it is remembered, interpreted, kept and passed on. `HI.K5`
