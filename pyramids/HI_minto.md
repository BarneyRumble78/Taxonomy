# HI — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Historians study the human past through traces, periods, explanations, long-run developments and the ways the past is remembered.

**Complication.** The field's sources mix subject matter with catalogue headings, duplicate entries and catch-all categories, so the underlying logic of what history asserts is hidden.

**Question.** How is everything in history divided by what it asserts about the human past?

**Answer.** History's objects divide by what a statement asserts about the human past, running from the single fact to its meaning: occurrence, time, cause, change and continuity, and meaning and memory.

## Governing thought

History's objects divide by what a statement asserts about the human past, running from the single fact to its meaning: occurrence, time, cause, change and continuity, and meaning and memory.

## Key line

Group of assertions about the human past. Basis: cause_effect. Dimension: what the sentence asserts about the human past. Order: time. The pillars run from the single established fact to the meaning later given to it.

1. Occurrence is the assertion that something happened, established from the material and documentary traces left by people. `HI.K1`
2. Time is the assertion that the past can be placed in dated eras, calendars and successive periods. `HI.K2`
3. Cause is the assertion that the past is explained by particular forces, such as power, social structures and human concerns, that made events happen. `HI.K3`
4. Change and continuity is the assertion that the human past moves and persists across peoples, regions, borders and generations as a connected whole. `HI.K4`
5. Meaning and memory is the assertion that the past is remembered, interpreted, kept and passed on to living audiences. `HI.K5`
