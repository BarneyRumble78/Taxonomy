# EA — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Earth science knowledge spans rocks, planetary forces, geological history, the surface envelopes and human use of space.

**Complication.** The sources mix subject matter with data services, instrument descriptions and duplicated headings, so topics sit in scattered groups.

**Question.** How is everything in Earth science divided?

**Answer.** Earth science claims are divided by what the claim is about, running from rock to settlement: materials, internal motion, deep time, surface and fluid envelopes, and human spatial organization.

## Governing thought

Earth science claims are divided by what the claim is about, running from rock to settlement: materials, internal motion, deep time, surface and fluid envelopes, and human spatial organization.

## Key line

Group of subjects of Earth claims. Basis: kinds. Dimension: what the claim is about. Order: structure. The pillars run from the solid rock of the planet outward through its motion, history and fluid surface to the human settlements on it.

1. Materials are what the Earth is made of, namely the chemistry, minerals, rocks and fluids that compose it. `EA.K1`
2. Internal motion is the force, field and movement within the solid Earth that deforms, heats and shakes it. `EA.K2`
3. Deep time is the history of the Earth, covering the age, layering, life and climate records preserved in its materials. `EA.K3`
4. Surface and fluid envelopes are the land surface, ice, waters and air that wrap the solid Earth, together with the means of observing them. `EA.K4`
5. Human spatial organization is how people locate, map, settle, use and manage places, and how they organize information about them. `EA.K5`
