# ED — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Education is studied through many overlapping vocabularies describing content, learners, teaching, testing and school systems.

**Complication.** The groups repeat the same topics under different headings and mix elements of an arrangement within single groups.

**Question.** How is everything in education divided?

**Answer.** Education is divided by the element of an educational arrangement, running from purpose to system: aims and curricula, learners, teaching, assessment, and institutions and systems.

## Governing thought

Education is divided by the element of an educational arrangement, running from purpose to system: aims and curricula, learners, teaching, assessment, and institutions and systems.

## Key line

Group of elements. Basis: cause_effect. Dimension: the element of an educational arrangement. Order: time. The pillars run from the purpose of education through those taught and the act of teaching to judging results and the system that sustains it.

1. Aims and curricula are the element of an educational arrangement that sets what is to be learned, through subject content, programs, frameworks and the perspectives that frame them. `ED.K1`
2. Learners are the element of an educational arrangement that consists of the people taught, their inner attributes, circumstances, social background and the support they receive. `ED.K2`
3. Teaching is the element of an educational arrangement that carries instruction out, through methods, organization, materials, and enrichment or remediation. `ED.K3`
4. Assessment is the element of an educational arrangement that judges learning and instruction, through measurement concepts, instruments, evaluation practice and research method. `ED.K4`
5. Institutions and systems are the element of an educational arrangement that organizes, governs, resources and connects education to society, through governance, resources, finance and work and society links. `ED.K5`
