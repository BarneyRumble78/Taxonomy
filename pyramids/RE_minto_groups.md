# RE Minto groups

## RE.O.A1
- **Religion and theology entries in the classification scheme divide into those naming theology as a discipline and those covering religion as a subject of thought.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds are arranged by structure, from the theology discipline entries to the entries on religion as a subject of thought._
  - **Theology entries in the ANZSRC scheme name the discipline of theology at the field level, its main subfield, and a residual category for unlisted topics.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure within the ANZSRC hierarchy: field, subfield, then residual category._
    - Theology
    - Theology
    - Theology not elsewhere classified
  - **Entries on religion as a subject of thought cover rational inquiry into religion, the general class of religions and mythology, and the doctrines of a faith.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure from the specific inquiry to the broad class and then the doctrinal subclass._
    - Philosophy of religion
    - Religions. Mythology. Rationalism
    - Doctrinal Theology

## RE.O.A2
- **Religious law can be understood through two kinds of subject matter: the general foundations common to religious legal thought and the specific legal traditions of particular faiths.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from general foundations to specific traditions._
  - **General religious foundations are the sacred text and the overall field of religious law that underlie particular legal traditions.**
    _basis: parts, order: structure. Ordered from the scriptural source to the general legal field._
    - The Bible
    - Religious law in general. Comparative religious law. Jurisprudence
  - **Specific religious legal traditions are the particular systems of law developed within the Jewish and Islamic faiths.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by tradition, Jewish law followed by Islamic law._
    - Jewish law
    - Islamic law

## RE.O.A3
- **Practical Theology is the field of religious study that deals with the practice of faith, including worship, ministry, and pastoral work.**
  _basis: parts, order: structure. Parts are listed in structural order of the field's practice areas._
  - **Practical Theology is the field of religious study that deals with the practice of faith, including worship, ministry, and pastoral work.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Single member, so no ranking applies._
    - Practical Theology

## RE.O.A4
- **Religious studies classifications are divided into four families according to which tradition or general approach to religion each one concerns.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure: specific traditions first, then general and cross-cutting approaches._
  - **Christian studies and its denominations and church law form the group of classifications about the Christian tradition.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from general Christianity to denominations to church law._
    - Christian studies
    - Christianity
    - Christian Denominations
    - History of canon law
    - Law of the Roman Catholic Church. The Holy See
  - **Jewish studies and Judaism form the group of classifications about the Jewish tradition.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the research field to the library class._
    - Jewish studies
    - Judaism
    - Islamic studies
    - Islam. Bahaism. Theosophy, etc.
  - **Eastern, other and general approaches to religion form the group of classifications that are not specific to Christianity, Judaism or Islam.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the overall field to specific traditions to residual categories._
    - Religious studies
    - Comparative religious studies
    - Studies in eastern religious traditions
    - Studies in religious traditions (excl. Eastern, Jewish, Christian and Islamic traditions)
    - Religious studies not elsewhere classified
  - **Buddhism and the study of religion, society and culture are concepts concerned with religious belief and practice and their place in human life.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure_
    - Buddhism
    - Religion, society and culture
