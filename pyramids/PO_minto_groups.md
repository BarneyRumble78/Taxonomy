# PO Minto groups

## PO.O.A1
- **The claim's subject, power, can be divided into three kinds according to the aspect of power each group concerns: who holds it, how it is exercised, and how it is justified or contested.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from the holders of power, to the practice of governing, to the theories that justify or contest power._
  - **Institutions and bodies that hold or embody political power at different levels of authority.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the supranational body to the national legislature and the general category of political institutions._
    - World government
    - Parliament
    - Political institutions
  - **Concepts concerning how power is exercised and administered in governing a political community.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from government as such to its policy, administration, and self-rule._
    - Government
    - Government policy
    - Public administration
    - Self government
  - **Theoretical and ideological frameworks that justify, contest, or define the status of power and the individual under it.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from general political theory to specific ideologies and the citizen's standing._
    - Political theory
    - Socialism. Communism. Anarchism
    - Citizenship

## PO.O.A2
- **The aspect of power a claim concerns can be its organization, its contestation, its foundations and conditions, its study, or its expression in particular settings.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds are listed in structural order, with no ranking by degree._
  - **Organization of power concerns the institutions, regimes, territorial arrangements and routes to office through which political authority is structured and distributed.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure_
    - **Formal governing institutions are the offices and bodies that hold and exercise state power through law, legislation and executive authority.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the whole state to its separate branches._
      - State
      - Law and Courts
      - Legislative Studies
      - Presidents and Executive Politics
      - Monarchy
    - **Territorial distribution of power concerns how authority is divided, shared or contested across levels and regions of a polity.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the shared federal structure to local and breakaway claims._
      - Federation
      - Federalism
      - Urban and Local Politics
      - Separatism
    - **Mechanisms for gaining and representing power are the organizations, elections and leaders through which citizens seek and confer authority.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from organizations to the leaders they produce._
      - Political Organizations and Parties
      - Political parties
      - Representation and Electoral Systems
      - Political leadership
    - **Organization and distribution of power concerns how authority is structured, shared and held across political forms and territories.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds of structure for holding authority, not ranked._
      - Politics
      - Republic
      - Pluralism
      - Regionalism
    - **Political systems and regimes are kinds of order that differ in how widely power is shared or concentrated, from democracy to totalitarianism.**
      _basis: kinds, order: degree. Ranked from most dispersed power (democracy) to most concentrated power (totalitarianism)._
      - Political systems
      - Democracy
      - Dictatorship
      - Totalitarianism
  - **Contestation of power concerns the protest, conflict, domination and resistance through which groups challenge, win, overturn or restrain political authority.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure_
    - **Contestation of power covers the protest, armed struggle and conflict by which groups challenge authority or wage war across borders.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from domestic protest to armed and international conflict._
      - Protest movements
      - Guerrilla activities
      - Polemology
      - Internationalism
    - **Challenges and resistance to power concern how established authority is opposed, overthrown, rejected or restrained through force or its refusal.**
      _basis: kinds, order: degree. Ranked from most violent opposition to power (revolution, anarchism) to least (nonviolence, pacifism)._
      - Revolutionary movements
      - Anarchism
      - Nonviolence
      - Pacifism
    - **Contests over power are the processes through which groups mobilise, struggle for, and destabilise political authority.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from organised collective action to individual conduct and open crisis._
      - Political movements
      - Internal politics
      - Political behaviour
      - Political crises
    - **Domination and resistance concern power imposed over peoples and the struggles against it, from empire and oppression to liberation and independence.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from imposition of domination to resistance and its outcome._
      - Imperialism
      - Colonial countries
      - Oppression
      - Liberation movements
      - Newly independent states
    - **Riots and electoral systems are two routes by which political power is contested and won, one through disorderly unrest and the other through formal voting procedures.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from informal contestation to formal procedure._
      - Riots
      - Electoral systems
  - **Foundations and conditions of power are the ideas, ideals, material resources and economic forces that justify, limit and enable political authority.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure_
    - **Foundations and study of power are the ideas, beliefs and methods used to justify, interpret and analyze political authority.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from normative theory to belief and empirical method._
      - Political philosophy
      - Religion and Politics
      - Political Methodology
    - **Normative and cultural dimensions of power concern the ideals, rights, history and imagery by which power is justified, limited or represented.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds of normative and cultural framing, not ranked._
      - Utopia
      - Human Rights
      - Politics and History
      - Politics, Literature, and the Arts
    - **Ideological and transformative power concerns the ideas, identities and upheavals that justify, bound or overturn political authority.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from ideas of authority to identity and upheaval._
      - Liberalism
      - Political theory
      - Revolutions
      - Nationalism and Politics
      - Migration and Citizenship
    - **Ideologies of power are the bodies of belief that justify how political authority should be organised and used.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from general doctrine to specific named ideologies._
      - Political doctrines
      - Marxism
      - Conservatism
      - Nationalism
    - **Political power and the systems of feudal and expert rule show how authority is organized and who holds it in society.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the general concept of power to specific forms of rule._
      - Political power
      - Feudalism
      - Technocracy
  - **Study of power covers the disciplines, methods, settings and channels through which political life is analysed, taught and communicated.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure_
    - **Study of power concerns the settings, identities and methods through which power is examined and taught as a field.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds of scholarly approach, not ranked._
      - European Politics and Society
      - Political Science Education
      - Qualitative and Multi-Method Research
      - Sexuality and Politics
    - **Power in government is examined through the general discipline of political science and its comparative and wider-regional subfields.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from the whole discipline to comparative and residual subfields._
      - Political science
      - Comparative government and politics
      - Government and politics of Asia and the Pacific
      - Political science not elsewhere classified
    - **Power is analysed through the relations and methods by which citizens and actors connect, participate and are studied.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from relational networks to research method to participation._
      - Political Networks
      - Experimental Research
      - Civic Engagement
    - **Scholarly and communicative study of power covers the disciplines and channels through which political life is analysed and conveyed.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the general discipline to its specialised branch and to communication._
      - Political science
      - Political sociology
      - Political communication
    - **The general scholarly study of politics, covering its theory, its institutions and public administration, and its legislative and executive papers, without limit to any one region.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from general papers to general theory to general institutions._
      - General legislative and executive papers
      - Political science (General)
      - Political institutions and public administration
  - **Power within particular settings is examined through the politics, institutions and administration of specific countries and world regions.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure_
    - **Power exercised within particular national or regional political systems is studied through country and region specific politics.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from national systems to world regions._
      - Canadian Politics
      - African Politics Conference Group
      - Middle East and North Africa Politics
      - Australian government and politics
      - New Zealand government and politics
    - **Each class covers the institutions and public administration through which political power is organized and exercised, divided by the world region it concerns.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by region from North America outward to Europe and Asia and Africa._
      - Political institutions and public administration (North America)
      - Political institutions and public administration (United States)
      - Political institutions and public administration (Canada, Latin
      - Political institutions and public administration (Europe)
      - Political institutions and public administration (Asia, Africa,
    - **Economic power is concerned with how class, capital and collective ownership distribute resources and advantage.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure, from class inequality to economic systems._
      - Class and Inequality
      - Capitalism
      - Collectivism
    - **Material and technological conditions of power concern how science, information, economy and coercive force shape what power can do.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds of material conditions, not ranked._
      - Science, Technology & Environmental Politics
      - Information Technology and Politics
      - Political Economy
      - Militarism

## PO.O.A3
- **Fields that study political power are classed by the aspect of power a claim concerns, either its social structuring by gender and race or its pursuit through persuasion, cognition, and voting.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure_
  - **Fields that study power as it is structured by gender and by race or ethnicity in politics.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds of identity-based power inquiry, ordered by structure; no ranking implied._
    - Women, Gender, and Politics Research
    - Gender and politics
    - Race, Ethnicity and Politics
  - **Fields that study how power is won and exercised through persuasion, mental processes, and citizens' electoral choices.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds of persuasion- and choice-based power inquiry, ordered by structure; no ranking implied._
    - Political Communication
    - Political Psychology
    - Elections, Public Opinion, and Voting Behavior

## PO.O.A4
- **Claims about power can be sorted by the aspect of power they concern, either its general exercise through government or its exercise within a particular policy sector.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the general exercise of power to its sector-specific exercise._
  - **General administrative and policy power is the authority exercised through governmental bodies in making and implementing public policy, whatever the policy domain.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Not ranked; these members state the general concept of administrative and policy power._
    - **This group covers the general study of how public authority is exercised through policy making and administration, whatever the policy domain.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from the broadest field category to its core subfields._
      - Public Policy
      - Public Administration
      - Policy and administration
      - Public administration
      - Public policy
    - **Administrative and policy power is the authority exercised through governmental bodies and the sector policies they design and implement.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from general administration to specific policy sectors and local government._
      - Policy and administration not elsewhere classified
      - Education Politics and Policy
      - Colonies and colonization. Emigration and immigration.
    - **Administrative and policy power is the authority exercised through governmental bodies and the sector policies they design and implement.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure_
      - Local government. Municipal government
      - Environmental politics
  - **Sector-specific policy power is the authority exercised over a particular domain of social, economic, security or informational life, such as welfare, development, order or resources.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Not ranked; each member is a distinct policy domain over which power is exercised._
    - **This group covers power exercised over social welfare and bodily wellbeing, including health, gender, housing and general social provision.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure across social-welfare policy domains._
      - Health Politics and Policy
      - Health policy
      - Gender, policy and administration
      - Housing policy
      - Social policy
    - **This group covers power exercised over economic activity, place and development, including economic development, tourism and urban policy.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure across economic and spatial policy domains._
      - Economic development policy
      - Tourism policy
      - Urban policy
      - State Politics and Policy
    - **This group covers power exercised over security, order and external relations, including crime policy, risk policy and foreign policy.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure across order and security policy domains._
      - Foreign Policy
      - Crime policy
      - Risk policy
    - **This group covers power exercised over knowledge, information and natural resources, including media, science and technology, and environment policy.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered by structure across knowledge and environment policy domains._
      - Communications and media policy
      - Research, science and technology policy
      - Environment policy

## PO.O.A5
- **Claims about power divide by which aspect of power they concern, either domestic authority within political systems or interstate relations in the international arena.**
  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Kinds are distinguished by the aspect of power the claim concerns, not ranked by degree._
  - **Domestic power concerns how authority is divided, shared and compared across governments within and among political systems.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from intergovernmental division of authority to cross-system comparison._
    - Federalism & Intergovernmental Relations
    - Comparative Politics
  - **Interstate power concerns how states relate, compete, cooperate and shape one another in the international arena.**
    _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered from historical foundations to the general field to cooperation._
    - International History and Politics
    - International relations
    - International relations
    - International Collaboration
