Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Language is studied through its sounds, writing, structure, meanings, social uses and the many languages and families of the world.

**Complication.** The existing groups mix subject matter with source schemes, so many items sit in catch-all or regional lists.

**Question.** How is everything about language divided?

**Answer.** Language divides into five aspects of its own: the signal that carries it, its form, its meaning, its variation in use, and the diversity of its languages and families.

## Governing thought

Language divides into five aspects of its own: the signal that carries it, its form, its meaning, its variation in use, and the diversity of its languages and families.

## Key line

Group of aspects of language. Basis: kinds. Dimension: the aspect of language the sentence is about. Order: structure. The aspects run from the physical signal through form, meaning and use to the whole family of languages.

1. Signal is the aspect of language concerned with the physical sounds and written marks that carry it. `LN.K1`
2. Form is the aspect of language concerned with its internal structure and the scholarly means used to describe it. `LN.K2`
3. Meaning is the aspect of language concerned with what words and sentences express and how that is recorded and used in context. `LN.K3`
4. Variation in use is the aspect of language concerned with how it differs and is shared among people, across communities and through media. `LN.K4`
5. Diversity is the aspect of language concerned with the many languages and families of the world, grouped by kind and region. `LN.K5`
