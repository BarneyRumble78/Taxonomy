# BU — Business, trade, tax and accounting

Standard v2 cited, not copied. Diagnosis: audit_mind_econ_business.md. v1 source: twelve.txt 3730–3917.

## 1. SCQA
**Situation.** Business knowledge states how firms record, finance, organise and sell, and how they trade and pay tax across borders.
**Complication.** v1 mixed practice with theory. It said Incoterms allocate title (they never do), confused VAT with sales tax, and shared finance theorems with EC.
**Question.** By what dimension can each business claim be placed once, with economic theory in EC and legal duty in LA?
**Answer.** By the practice of the firm the claim is about: its accounts, its finance, its operations and organisation, its marketing, and its trade and tax.

## 4. Face O — Objects

### BU.O.A1 Accounts
**SCQA.** A firm first keeps a record of what it owns, owes and earns. The rules for the record are conventions, set by standard setters. What do accounts claim, and under which rules? Key line: the record, reporting standards, management accounting, audit.
- BU.O.A1.1 The record — double entry: every transaction posts equal debits and credits, so assets = liabilities + equity. This is an identity of the convention (W12), not an empirical law.
- BU.O.A1.2 Reporting standards — IFRS (IASB) and US GAAP (FASB). They differ, for example in that US GAAP permits LIFO inventory and IFRS does not.
- BU.O.A1.3 Management accounting — costing, budgeting, variance analysis.
- BU.O.A1.4 Audit and assurance — an audit gives reasonable, not absolute, assurance that statements are free of material misstatement (W11).

### BU.O.A2 Finance
**SCQA.** Firms raise and invest capital at a price set by markets. How should a firm value projects and choose its funding? Key line: valuation, capital structure, risk and return, derivatives.
- BU.O.A2.1 Valuation — net present value at the opportunity cost of capital. Discounted cash flow assumes the forecast and the rate.
- BU.O.A2.2 Capital structure — Modigliani–Miller: with no taxes, no bankruptcy costs, symmetric information and perfect capital markets, firm value does not depend on the debt–equity mix. With corporate tax, debt adds a tax shield. Theorem MA.B.C4; use BU.
- BU.O.A2.3 Risk and return — CAPM: with mean–variance investors, homogeneous expectations, frictionless markets and unlimited risk-free borrowing and lending, expected excess return is linear in beta. Empirical support is weak (W4 defeaters).
- BU.O.A2.4 Derivatives — Black–Scholes prices a European option assuming log-normal prices, constant volatility and rate, continuous frictionless trading and no dividends. Theorem MA.B.C4; use BU.
- BU.O.A2.5 Market efficiency — the efficient-market hypothesis comes in weak, semi-strong and strong forms. Tests face the joint-hypothesis problem: each needs a model of normal returns.

### BU.O.A3 Operations and organisation
**SCQA.** Firms turn inputs into outputs through people and processes. What makes operations and organisations perform? Key line: operations, supply chains, organisation design, people management.
- BU.O.A3.1 Operations — Little's law L = λW holds for any stable queueing system in steady state (theorem MA.O.A5).
- BU.O.A3.2 Supply chains — inventory, logistics. The bullwhip effect amplifies demand variance upstream.
- BU.O.A3.3 Organisation design — structure, governance, agency. The agency problem in principal–agent form is EC.O.A2.3; its corporate remedy is BU.
- BU.O.A3.4 People management — human resources and industrial relations. Group behaviour is MS.
- BU.O.A3.5 Entrepreneurship and innovation — founding and scaling firms. A rival's response to a move is ST.

### BU.O.A4 Marketing
**SCQA.** Firms must find and keep customers. What do marketing claims state? Key line: customers, products and brands, pricing practice, channels.
- BU.O.A4.1 Customers — segmentation, consumer behaviour. The cognition is MS.
- BU.O.A4.2 Products and brands.
- BU.O.A4.3 Pricing practice — list prices, discounts. Market equilibrium price is EC.
- BU.O.A4.4 Channels — retail, digital, distribution.

### BU.O.A5 Trade and tax
**SCQA.** Firms sell across borders and owe tax. What does the firm's practice of trade and tax claim? Key line: trade terms, customs, tax computation, transfer pricing.
- BU.O.A5.1 Trade terms — Incoterms 2020 (ICC, eleven rules) allocate delivery tasks, costs and the passing of risk between seller and buyer. They do not allocate title. Title passes under the sale contract and the governing law (LA).
- BU.O.A5.2 Customs and trade compliance — tariff classification (Harmonized System, W12), rules of origin.
- BU.O.A5.3 Tax computation — a VAT is charged at each stage on the value added, with credit for input tax. A retail sales tax is charged once at the final sale. New Zealand GST is a VAT-type tax (15%).
- BU.O.A5.4 Transfer pricing — the arm's-length principle (OECD guidelines, W12). Its legal force is LA.
- BU.O.A5.5 Payments and trade finance — letters of credit (UCP 600, W12).

## 8. Assignment rules
1. Place by the firm's practice.
2. The finance theorem is MA (MSC 91G); its use by the firm is BU; the market price of risk is EC.
3. The incidence of a tax is EC, the duty is LA, the computation is BU.
4. Title to goods is LA; Incoterms allocate risk and costs (BU).
5. A rival's strategic response is ST.
6. Behaviour of people inside firms is MS; the firm's management practice is BU.
