# AG — Agriculture, veterinary and food

New field, added at the owner's direction. Standard v2 cited, not copied. Why it is here: ANZSRC 30 had no core home. A crop is not only a species, an animal disorder is not human medicine, and a food standard is not only chemistry.

## 1. SCQA
**Situation.** Agricultural, veterinary and food sciences state how organisms are produced and kept for human use, how their health is maintained, and how food is made safe.
**Complication.** v1 spread these across BI, CH and MD. "Yield of this cultivar under this management" had no home.
**Question.** By what dimension can each claim about producing and keeping organisms and food be placed once?
**Answer.** By the stage of the production system the claim is about: the organism bred, its cultivation or husbandry, its health, the food made from it, and the farming system.

## 2. Governing thought
Agriculture is exhausted by four faces of one pyramid: objects, methods, warrants and bridges.
Its objects divide into five stages of production: breeding, cultivation and husbandry, crop and animal health, food, and farming systems.

## 3. Key line
Dimension: **the stage of the production system.** Order: from the organism to the whole system.
1. Breeding
2. Cultivation and husbandry
3. Crop and animal health
4. Food
5. Farming systems

## 4. Face O — Objects

### AG.O.A1 Breeding
**SCQA.** Production starts with chosen organisms. How are they improved? Key line: selection, genetic improvement, genetic resources.
- AG.O.A1.1 Selection — the breeder's equation R = h²S predicts response to selection, assuming additive inheritance and a stable environment. The genetics is BI.
- AG.O.A1.2 Genetic improvement — hybrids, marker-assisted selection, gene editing. Regulation is LA.
- AG.O.A1.3 Genetic resources — seed banks, landraces.

### AG.O.A2 Cultivation and husbandry
**SCQA.** Organisms are grown and kept under managed conditions. What management gives what result? Key line: soils and nutrients, water, crop management, animal husbandry, aquaculture and forestry.
- AG.O.A2.1 Soils and nutrients — fertiliser response. Liebig's law of the minimum holds as a first approximation, when one nutrient limits growth. The soil's state is EA.
- AG.O.A2.2 Water and irrigation.
- AG.O.A2.3 Crop management — rotation, tillage, sowing. A yield claim must state season, site and population.
- AG.O.A2.4 Animal husbandry — pastoral and housed systems, nutrition, welfare. NZ pastoral dairying is a substrate.
- AG.O.A2.5 Aquaculture, fisheries and forestry production.

### AG.O.A3 Crop and animal health
**SCQA.** Producing organisms fall ill and are attacked. How is their health kept? Key line: plant protection, veterinary medicine, biosecurity, zoonoses.
- AG.O.A3.1 Plant protection — pests, weeds, pathogens; integrated pest management.
- AG.O.A3.2 Veterinary medicine — diagnosis and treatment of animal disorders.
- AG.O.A3.3 Biosecurity — border and incursion management (e.g. Mycoplasma bovis in NZ from 2017, UNVERIFIED date).
- AG.O.A3.4 Zoonoses — the animal side is AG; human disease is MD (One Health is a bridge).

### AG.O.A4 Food
**SCQA.** Produce becomes food. What makes food safe, nutritious and stable? Key line: composition and nutrition, processing, safety, standards.
- AG.O.A4.1 Composition and nutrition — the molecule is CH; human nutritional need is MD.
- AG.O.A4.2 Processing and preservation — fermentation, pasteurisation, drying.
- AG.O.A4.3 Food safety — HACCP as a control system (W11); pathogen limits.
- AG.O.A4.4 Food standards — Codex Alimentarius; FSANZ for Australia and NZ (W12; legal force LA).

### AG.O.A5 Farming systems
**SCQA.** Farms are systems of land, organisms, labour and markets. Key line: system productivity, farm management, environmental effect.
- AG.O.A5.1 System productivity — total factor productivity of farms (measurement here; economics EC).
- AG.O.A5.2 Farm management — decisions under weather and price risk.
- AG.O.A5.3 Environmental effect of farming — nitrate leaching, methane. The impact claim is EV; AG owns the practice that changes it.

## 8. Assignment rules
1. Place by the stage of production.
2. The taxon and its biology are BI. Its production is AG.
3. An animal disorder is AG; a human disorder is MD.
4. The molecule is CH; the food's safety and standard are AG.
5. Farm economics and prices are EC; environmental impact is EV.
