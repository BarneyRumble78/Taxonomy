## Introduction

**Situation.** Law's objects are the legal relations that rules create, enforce and adjudicate between persons and states.

**Complication.** Existing schemes mix subject-matter fields with catalogue headings, residual classes and duplicate topics, so the same relation appears in several places.

**Question.** How is everything in law divided?

**Answer.** Law is divided by the legal relation a claim states, into sources, obligations, wrongs and remedies, public power, and forums and proof, running from where a rule gets its validity to how a dispute is decided.

## Governing thought

Law is divided by the legal relation a claim states, into sources, obligations, wrongs and remedies, public power, and forums and proof, running from where a rule gets its validity to how a dispute is decided.

## Key line

Group of legal relations. Basis: cause_effect. Dimension: the legal relation the claim states. Order: time. The pillars run from the validity of a rule through the duties it creates, their breach and the state's power, to the forum that decides.

1. Sources are the relation in which a legal rule gains its validity through making, reform, theory or the norms binding states. `LA.K1`
2. Obligations are the relation in which persons owe duties and hold rights against one another in ownership, contract, status, trade and resource use. `LA.K2`
3. Wrongs and remedies are the relation in which a party answers for harm through liability, punishment or settlement. `LA.K3`
4. Public power is the relation in which the state holds and exercises authority over persons and owes them protections. `LA.K4`
5. Forums and proof are the relation in which a claim is brought before an institution and decided by set procedure. `LA.K5`
