Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Law is a large body of rules, institutions and practices that people use to govern relations among persons, states and things.

**Complication.** Existing schemes shelve law by library class, code or jurisdiction, which scatters claims that state the same kind of legal relation.

**Question.** How is everything in law divided by the legal relation each claim states?

**Answer.** Law divides along the legal relation a claim states into sources, obligations, wrongs and remedies, public power, and forums and proof, ordered from the validity of a rule to its decision.

## Governing thought

Law divides along the legal relation a claim states into sources, obligations, wrongs and remedies, public power, and forums and proof, ordered from the validity of a rule to its decision.

## Key line

Group of legal relations. Basis: cause_effect. Dimension: the legal relation the claim states. Order: time. The pillars run from where a rule gets its validity, through the duties and wrongs it creates and the public power that enforces it, to the forums that decide claims.

1. Sources are the legal relation in which a rule gains its validity, from made law and custom to theory and comparison of whole legal orders. `LA.K1`
2. Obligations are the legal relation in which private parties owe duties and hold rights against each other in trade, property and personal life. `LA.K2`
3. Wrongs and remedies are the legal relation in which a harm or offence is answered by liability, punishment or enforcement. `LA.K3`
4. Public power is the legal relation in which the state holds authority over persons, territory and resources and is limited by rights. `LA.K4`
5. Forums and proof are the legal relation in which claims are brought before institutions and decided through set procedure and advocacy. `LA.K5`
