Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Engineering builds artefacts whose behaviour must be predicted before they are made or used.

**Complication.** The field's topics come from several catalogues that mix vehicles, devices, processes and materials, so no single subject-matter order is visible.

**Question.** Which balance or closure completes the predictive model of each kind of artefact?

**Answer.** Engineering's object claims are divided by the quantity whose balance or constitutive closure finishes the predictive model of an artefact, running from load path to material: force and deformation, mass and momentum, energy and entropy, electromagnetic state, and composition and microstructure.

## Governing thought

Engineering's object claims are divided by the quantity whose balance or constitutive closure finishes the predictive model of an artefact, running from load path to material: force and deformation, mass and momentum, energy and entropy, electromagnetic state, and composition and microstructure.

## Key line

Group of balances. Basis: kinds. Dimension: the quantity whose balance or constitutive closure finishes the predictive model of an artefact. Order: structure. The pillars run from the load path of a structure to the material it is made of.

1. Force and deformation is the balance that closes the model of artefacts whose behaviour is set by load paths, fluid forces on bodies and the geometry and tolerance of the structure. `EN.K1`
2. Mass and momentum is the balance that closes the model of artefacts whose behaviour is set by flowing fluids, reacting species, separated streams and material removed or formed. `EN.K2`
3. Energy and entropy is the balance that closes the model of artefacts whose behaviour is set by the conversion, transfer and dissipation of energy. `EN.K3`
4. Electromagnetic state is the balance that closes the model of artefacts whose behaviour is set by charge, field, signal and power quantities. `EN.K4`
5. Composition and microstructure is the closure that completes the model of artefacts through the constitutive properties of the substances and nanostructures they are made from. `EN.K5`
