# MD — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Medical knowledge spans disease classifications, biomedical sciences, specialties, therapies and public health, each catalogued on its own scheme.

**Complication.** These schemes mix organs, agents, methods and settings, so the same clinical question is scattered and some groups are generic catch-alls.

**Question.** How is everything in medicine divided when organised by the clinical question asked of a disorder?

**Answer.** Medicine's knowledge is divided by the clinical question asked of a disorder, in the order of the clinical sequence: the disorders themselves, their causes and mechanisms, their diagnosis and prognosis, their treatment and drugs, and the populations and prevention in which they are met.

## Governing thought

Medicine's knowledge is divided by the clinical question asked of a disorder, in the order of the clinical sequence: the disorders themselves, their causes and mechanisms, their diagnosis and prognosis, their treatment and drugs, and the populations and prevention in which they are met.

## Key line

Group of clinical questions. Basis: cause_effect. Dimension: the clinical question asked of a disorder. Order: time. The pillars follow the clinical sequence from recognising a disorder through explaining, identifying and treating it to preventing it in populations.

1. Disorders are the clinical question of what the condition is, grouped by the organ system, regulatory process, mental function or external cause affected. `MD.K1`
2. Causes and mechanisms are the clinical question of why a disorder arises, covering immune, infectious, biochemical and physiological explanations. `MD.K2`
3. Diagnosis and prognosis are the clinical question of how a disorder is detected, identified and its course judged. `MD.K3`
4. Treatment and drugs are the clinical question of how a disorder is managed, by medicines, procedures, therapy and care. `MD.K4`
5. Populations and prevention are the clinical question of how a disorder is distributed, prevented and cared for across groups of people. `MD.K5`
