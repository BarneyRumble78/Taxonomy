# MD — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Clinicians meet disorders as many kinds of illness affecting body systems and the mind.

**Complication.** The same disorder can be approached through its nature, cause, detection, treatment or population spread, and sources mix these views.

**Question.** How is everything in medicine divided?

**Answer.** Medicine is divided by the clinical question asked of a disorder, in the order of the clinical sequence: the disorders themselves, their causes and mechanisms, their diagnosis and prognosis, their treatment and drugs, and the populations and prevention in which they occur.

## Governing thought

Medicine is divided by the clinical question asked of a disorder, in the order of the clinical sequence: the disorders themselves, their causes and mechanisms, their diagnosis and prognosis, their treatment and drugs, and the populations and prevention in which they occur.

## Key line

Group of clinical questions. Basis: cause_effect. Dimension: the clinical question asked of a disorder. Order: time. The questions follow the clinical sequence, from recognising the disorder through explaining, identifying and treating it to preventing it in populations.

1. Disorders is the clinical question of what illness is present, grouping the kinds of disease by body system, disease class and mental function. `MD.K1`
2. Causes and mechanisms is the clinical question of why a disorder arises, covering immune responses, body systems and molecular agents, and the viruses that cause disease. `MD.K2`
3. Diagnosis and prognosis is the clinical question of what a disorder is and how it is detected and judged, through investigation, measurement and assessment. `MD.K3`
4. Treatment and drugs is the clinical question of how a disorder is managed, through physical treatment, patient support, medicines, biotechnology, dental care and rehabilitation. `MD.K4`
5. Populations and prevention is the clinical question of how a disorder is distributed, prevented and cared for across communities, through nutrition, epidemiology, patient groups, health systems and public health. `MD.K5`

### 2. Causes and mechanisms is the clinical question of why a disorder arises, covering immune responses, body systems and molecular agents, and the viruses that cause disease.

  _basis: kinds, order: structure. Groups run from foundational concepts through response events to regulation and dysfunction._

      - Innate immunity
      - Immunogenetics (incl. genetic immunology)
    - **Immune intervention concepts ask how the immune system can be applied or manipulated to treat disease, transplant tissue or attack tumours.**
      _basis: kinds, order: structure. Ordered structurally from general applied methods to transplant and tumour settings._
      - Applied immunology (incl. antibody engineering, xenotransplantation and t-cell therapies)
      - Transplantation immunology
      - Tumour immunology
