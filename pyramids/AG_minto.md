# AG — top-down Minto pyramid

Built by scripts/minto_pass.py from the grouped classification. Three rules (Minto pp. 9-11): each summary states the idea of its group, members are the same kind of idea, and they are in a stated order.

## Introduction

**Situation.** Agriculture produces crops, animals, fish, trees and food through a sequence of stages.

**Complication.** The groups mix sources and stages, so many overlap or sit at different levels.

**Question.** How is everything in agriculture divided?

**Answer.** Agriculture is divided by the stage of the production system, from the organism to the whole system: breeding, cultivation and husbandry, crop and animal health, food, and farming systems.

## Governing thought

Agriculture is divided by the stage of the production system, from the organism to the whole system: breeding, cultivation and husbandry, crop and animal health, food, and farming systems.

## Key line

Group of stages. Basis: cause_effect. Dimension: the stage of the production system. Order: time. Stages run from the organism that is bred, through its raising and protection, to the food produced and the whole farming system.

1. Breeding is the stage that creates, improves and classifies the organisms, whether plants, animals or microbes, that production starts from. `AG.K1`
2. Cultivation and husbandry is the stage in which crops, trees, animals and fish are established, fed and grown using inputs and field practices. `AG.K2`
3. Crop and animal health is the stage that protects production from disease, stress and decline and detects problems early. `AG.K3`
4. Food is the stage in which production outputs are processed, stored, valued and distributed as products. `AG.K4`
5. Farming systems are the whole production systems, with their management, people, institutions, analysis and change, that frame all other stages. `AG.K5`
