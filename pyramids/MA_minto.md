## Introduction

**Situation.** Mathematics spans a very large body of specialised areas that were published under several different schemes.

**Complication.** The areas mix pure structures, computing methods, uses in other sciences and the history and teaching of the discipline, and some appear twice under different names.

**Question.** How is everything in mathematics divided?

**Answer.** Mathematics is divided by the role its knowledge plays into the structures it studies, the computation and inference it performs, the modelling of other fields' claims, and the discipline as a human activity.

## Governing thought

Mathematics is divided by the role its knowledge plays into the structures it studies, the computation and inference it performs, the modelling of other fields' claims, and the discipline as a human activity.

## Key line

Group of roles. Basis: parts. Dimension: role the knowledge plays. Order: structure. The roles run from pure structure, through computation and inference, to modelling of outside claims, and finally to the discipline as a human activity.

1. Structures are the role in which mathematics studies abstract objects such as algebraic systems, spaces, functions and the foundations of proof for their own sake. `MA.K1`
2. Computation and inference is the role in which mathematics computes results by algorithms and infers conclusions from chance and data. `MA.K2`
3. Modelling is the role in which mathematics expresses another field's claims about physical, living, social and decision systems in mathematical language. `MA.K3`
4. The discipline is the role in which mathematics is a human activity with a history, a teaching practice and a general literature. `MA.K4`
