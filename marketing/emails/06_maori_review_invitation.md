# Letter: invitation to lead the Māori-led review (send through an institution)

**Subject:** Invitation to lead a review of mātauranga Māori placements in a knowledge classification

Tēnā koe [Name],

I am writing to invite [unit or organisation] to consider leading a review of how a new classification of knowledge, called Taxonomy, treats mātauranga Māori.

Taxonomy was drafted outside te ao Māori. It contains a field for Indigenous knowledge and places some Māori concepts in other fields. These placements are provisional. Nothing about them will be promoted or published as final until a Māori-led panel has reviewed them.

What I am asking:
- Whether you would be willing to lead or convene that review, or advise who should.
- A panel chaired by Māori, with reviewers chosen by Māori.

What I offer:
- Paid time for reviewers, at rates you set.
- Control of anything shared in review, under Te Mana Raraunga and CARE principles.
- The right to move, refuse or withhold any placement, including rejecting the Indigenous field altogether.

The review pack is attached (19 placements, framed as questions). I would be grateful for any response, including "no".

Ngā mihi nui,
Chris Townsend
Whanganui
