## 2. Explainer, 3 minutes
1. **The problem (0:00–0:40).** AI text, briefs mixing evidence and opinion, schemes built for shelves.
2. **The idea (0:40–1:20).** Mathematics at the apex; 25 fields; four faces; the ownership rule shown on the tax example.
3. **The evidence types (1:20–1:50).** Thirteen warrants. "A court's finding does not settle a scientific fact."
4. **Three uses (1:50–2:40).** Classify; see the whole (sunglasses); relate two things (facial recognition and privacy).
5. **Call to act (2:40–3:00).** Try it; register for the API; tell me where it is wrong.

## 3. Talk, 10 minutes: "Mathematics first"
1. Why classification matters more when machines write.
2. Barbara Minto's pyramid principle, applied to knowledge.
3. The five cells of mathematics and the 63 MSC classes.
4. Extending to 24 fields: the ownership rule and borders.
5. Warrants and hallucination.
6. Security: indicators versus attribution; poisoned retrieval.
7. Indigenous knowledge and why it waits for review.
8. What is not yet proven: the human reliability study.
9. How to join.

## 4. Developer demo, 5 minutes
Terminal plus browser: `pip install` (when packaged), a classify call, the JSON response, a whole-view call, a RAG pipeline that filters by warrant, and the MCP server in Claude.
